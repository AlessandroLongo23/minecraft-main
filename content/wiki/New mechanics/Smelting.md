*Mechanic*

The **Furnace** is a [[The Base|Base]] station that refines raw drops into finished materials by burning fuel — exactly like its Minecraft counterpart.

## Building the Furnace
Craft it once at the [[Crafting]] table: the Minecraft ring of **8 [[Cobblestone]]** (a 3×3 with a hollow center). Crafting it unlocks the Furnace in [[The Base]]; from then on its "Open" button works.

## How it works
Open the Furnace and you get the classic two-slot layout:
- **Top slot** — drag in a raw ore to smelt.
- **Bottom slot** — drag in fuel.
- **Smelt** — produces one refined item per click; the output preview shows what you'll get.

Each smelt spends **1 fuel charge + 1 raw item → 1 refined item**. You can hold Smelt down: the slots auto-refill from your inventory as long as you have more.

### Fuel
Fuel burns into a charge buffer that carries between smelts:

| Fuel | Charges |
| --- | --- |
| 1 [[Coal]] | +4 |
| 1 [[Wood]] | +1 |

## Smelt recipes
| Raw input | Refined output |
| --- | --- |
| Raw Iron | [[Iron]] |
| Raw Gold | [[Gold]] |
| [[Sand]] | [[Glass]] |

These three outputs are **smelt-only** — Iron, Gold and Glass no longer drop, mine, or pack. You gather the raw form (Raw Iron / Raw Gold / Sand from [[Mining]] and [[Resource Drops|drops]]) and refine it here.

Smelting is gated by inventory room: if the refined item wouldn't fit, Smelt stays disabled so a raw item and its fuel are never wasted.

## What the refined materials feed
- **[[Iron]]** → tools, the [[Anvil]], [[Flint and Steel]].
- **[[Gold]]** → golden tools, brewing ingredients ([[Potions]]).
- **[[Glass]]** → the Glass [[Sheets|Sheet]] and Water Bottles (potions).
