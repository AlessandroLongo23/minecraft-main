*Mechanic*

Crafting is the heart of [[The Base]]: a Minecraft-style **3×3 grid** with **shaped** recipes that turns [[Resources|ores]] and intermediates into [[Tools]], consumables, jokers, more resources, and the other [[The Base|Base stations]] themselves.

## Opening the table
Open **[[The Base]]** from the resource hotbar, then **Crafting Table → Open**. The overlay has:
- **Recipes** list (left) — click a recipe to auto-fill it.
- **3×3 grid → output slot + Craft button** (right).
- **Inventory row** (middle) — your owned resources, draggable into the grid.
- **Back** button — closes and refunds any placed tiles.

## How matching works
Recipes are **shaped**, not shapeless: the grid and the recipe are both trimmed to their tight bounding box and compared cell-by-cell. The *shape* matters, but its absolute position in the 3×3 doesn't. There is **no rotation or mirroring**, and empty interior cells must stay empty. The first matching recipe wins.

You can craft a recipe two ways:
- **Click the recipe** — if you can afford it, the tiles auto-fill into the grid.
- **Drag** resources from the inventory row into the grid manually.

A craft only goes through when you can **afford** every ingredient, and:
- a **resource** output has room in your inventory,
- a **joker / consumable** output has a free card slot (no slot → a red "No Card Slots!" warning),
- a **station** output isn't already built (so you can't waste materials re-crafting one).

Completing any craft also grants [[Experience]].

## What you can craft
Crafting outputs four kinds of thing:

- **Resources / intermediates** — e.g. [[Sticks]], Paper, Book, Sugar, Blaze Powder, Water Bottle.
- **Tools (consumables)** — every [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] and [[Axe]] in Wood→Diamond, plus [[Torch]], [[Arrow]], [[Flint and Steel]], [[Eye of Ender]], [[TNT]], [[Firework]], Bone Meal, and the Glass [[Sheets|Sheet]].
- **Jokers** — the Archery cluster: **Bow**, **Fishing Rod**, **Crossbow** (the Crossbow needs you to already own a Bow).
- **Stations** — one-time unlocks that flip on in [[The Base]]: **Furnace** ([[Smelting]]), **Chest**, **[[Anvil]]**, **[[Composter]]**, **[[Brewing Stand]]**, **Enchanting Table** ([[Enchanting]]).

Material recipes come from each tool × its craftable materials. See each tool's page for its exact grid and per-material stats: [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]], [[Axe]], [[Torch]].

> **Netherite tools aren't crafted here.** As in Minecraft, you craft the **Diamond** tool, then **upgrade** it at the **[[Anvil]] → Upgrade** tab (Diamond tool + 1 [[Netherite]] → Netherite tool, keeping its enchants & durability).

## Related
The other stations live in [[The Base]]: [[Smelting]] (Furnace), [[Anvil]], [[Composter]], [[Brewing Stand]], and the Enchanting Table ([[Enchanting]]). Note the **Anvil** and **Enchanting Table** are separate stations — you craft books at the table and apply them at the Anvil. Metals you craft with mostly come from [[Mining]] + [[Smelting]].
