*Mechanic*

![[hearts.png|150]]

A Minecraft-style **health bar** of **10 hearts (20 HP)** — 1 HP is half a heart. It persists across blinds and antes, and resets to full at the start of each run.

## What damages you
- **Mob attacks** — when you **play a hand against a [[New Bosses|boss]] and fail to beat it while you still have hands left**, the mob deals damage. Your *final* failed hand is a normal game-over instead, so attacks only land when you have hands to spare. Per-mob damage:

| Boss | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| [[Creeper\|The Creeper]] | 6 ♥ (3 hearts) |
| [[Skeleton\|The Skeleton]], [[Husk\|The Husk]], [[Drowned\|The Drowned]], [[Stray\|The Stray]] | 3 ♥ (1.5 hearts) |
| All other bosses (default) | 2 ♥ (1 heart) |

- **Starvation** — while your [[Hunger]] is at 0, each blind costs **2 HP (1 heart)**.

## What heals you
- **Well-fed regen** — while [[Hunger]] is **≥ 14 (7 drumsticks)**, you heal **1 HP (½ heart) per blind**.
- Eating [[Food]] mainly restores hunger; staying well-fed is how you passively recover hearts.

## Dying
At **0 HP** the run ends (game over). Death by mob/starvation **bypasses Mr. Bones** — he only saves you from falling short on chips, not from running out of hearts.
