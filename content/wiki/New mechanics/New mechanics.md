BalaCraft layers several Minecraft survival systems on top of Balatro. Each has its own page:

| System | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [[Crafting]] | A 3×3 crafting table to turn [[Resources\|ores]] into [[Tools]] |
| [[Enchanting]] | Upgrade [[Tools]] with [[Enchanting Books]]; tools gain durability (Uses) |
| [[Health]] | A 10-heart health pool — mobs hurt you, starvation kills |
| [[Hunger]] | A 10-drumstick hunger bar drained by playing/skipping blinds, refilled by [[Food]] |
| [[Experience]] | An XP bar that fills as you play (Minecraft leveling curve); levels are spent on [[Enchanting]] |
| [[Villager Trading]] | Spend [[Emerald\|emeralds]] on resources & tools you can't buy in the shop |

Related content systems with their own sections:
- [[Resources]] — gathering ores from blinds & [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]
- [[Food]] — what you eat to manage [[Hunger]]
- [[Biomes]] — biomes & dimensions

## How it all fits together
1. **Beat blinds** → gather [[Resources|ores]] (drops scale with ante & [[Biomes|biome]]).
2. **Craft** ([[Crafting]]) ores + [[Sticks]] into [[Tools]] (Sword/Pickaxe/Shovel/Torch).
3. **Survive** — playing/skipping blinds drains [[Hunger]]; eat [[Food]] to stay fed (well-fed regenerates [[Health]]); failing boss hands costs hearts.
4. **Level up** — everything you do grants [[Experience]].
5. **Enchant** ([[Enchanting]]) — spend levels to apply [[Enchanting Books]] to your tools (unlocked by the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher).
