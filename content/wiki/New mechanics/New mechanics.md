BalaCraft layers a full stack of Minecraft survival systems on top of Balatro: you **gather** materials from blinds and the deck, **craft & smelt** them into tools and stations, then survive on hunger/health and progress through XP, enchanting, trading and dimensions. Each system has its own page.

---

## Gathering
| System | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [[Resources]] | The 30+ ores, mob drops and intermediates you collect, stored in a Minecraft inventory |
| [[Resource Drops]] | Where everything comes from per blind — Night/Cave/Boss pools, mob waves, dimension trickles (balance reference) |
| [[Mining]] | Use a [[Pickaxe]] or [[Axe]] on ore-block cards in the deck to mine metals (the main metal supply) |

## Crafting & stations ([[The Base]])
The Base is the hub for all crafting stations — open it from the resource hotbar.

| System | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [[Crafting]] | The 3×3 Crafting Table: ores + [[Sticks]] → [[Tools]], consumables, jokers, and the stations below |
| [[Smelting]] | The Furnace: smelt raw ores into ingots and Glass, burning fuel |
| [[Anvil]] | Combine tools, forge [[Sheets]], **apply enchant books to tools**, and upgrade Diamond → Netherite |
| [[Composter]] | Turn flowers & farm plants into Bone Meal |
| [[Brewing Stand]] | Brew [[Potions]] from dimension ingredients |
| [[The Base]] | The station hub itself (which stations exist, how each is unlocked) |

## Survival
| System | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [[Health]] | A 10-heart pool — mobs hurt you, starvation kills; banked saturation heals you |
| [[Hunger]] | A 10-drumstick bar drained by playing/skipping blinds, refilled by [[Food]] |

| System | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [[Experience]] | An XP bar on Minecraft's real leveling curve; levels are spent generating enchant books |
| [[Enchanting]] | Generate enchant books at the Enchanting Table, then apply them to [[Tools]] or playing cards |

| [[Dimensions]] | Travel the Overworld → Nether → End; each changes [[Biomes|biomes]] and drop pools |

## How it all fits together
1. **Beat blinds** → gather [[Resources|ores]] and mob drops ([[Resource Drops]]), and **[[Mining|mine]]** metals from deck ore-blocks. Drops scale with ante and [[Biomes|biome]].
2. **Build a Base** — craft a Furnace ([[Smelting]]), then a Chest, [[Anvil]], [[Composter]], [[Brewing Stand]] and Enchanting Table ([[The Base]]).
3. **Craft** ([[Crafting]]) ores + [[Sticks]] into [[Tools]] and consumables; smelt raw ores into ingots.
4. **Survive** — playing/skipping blinds drains [[Hunger]]; eat [[Food]] to stay fed (over-eating banks saturation that heals [[Health]]); failing boss hands costs hearts.
5. **Level up** — almost everything grants [[Experience]].
6. **Enchant** ([[Enchanting]]) — spend levels + [[Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]] at the Enchanting Table to make books, then apply them free at the [[Anvil]] (tools) or in hand (cards).
7. **Explore** — light a portal to reach the Nether, follow the Eye-of-Ender trail to the End ([[Dimensions]]).
