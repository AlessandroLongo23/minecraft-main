*Mechanic*

Mining is how you pull ores off your own deck. **Ore Block** cards appear on plain playing cards each blind; highlight them in hand and **Use a [[Pickaxe]]** (or an [[Axe]] for Wood) to break them and bank the ore into your hotbar.

## Where ore blocks come from
Every time you **select a blind**, fresh Ore Blocks are added to random plain cards in your deck (cards that have no enhancement yet). They **accumulate** — un-mined blocks stay on the card until you break them, so your deck steadily fills with ore.

How many, and which ores, scales with the run:
- **Ante** sets the depth (which tiers can appear). Tier 2 ores unlock around ante 3, tier 3 around ante 6, and tier 4 ([[Netherite]]) from ante 8.
- The active **[[Biomes|biome]]** biases *which* ore of a given tier you get (e.g. a Desert favors Gold/Iron blocks).

An Ore Block keeps the card's normal rank and suit — it adds no scoring effect, it's just a marker that there's ore to mine.

## Mining an ore
1. **Highlight** the Ore Block card(s) in your hand.
2. **Use** a [[Pickaxe]] (its Use button works on the selected cards, just like applying a Tarot).
3. Each block this pickaxe can break yields its ore, pops a "+N" message, and the card **reverts to a normal card**.

Mining is **multi-use** within a blind — you can keep mining with a [[Pickaxe]] (and chopping wood with an [[Axe]]) as many times as you like, each spending one durability use, until the tool breaks. (The [[Sword]], [[Shovel]] and the Axe's **weapon** mode are the once-per-blind ones.) A no-op click (nothing valid selected) is free — it won't waste a use.

### Wood is special
Wood blocks are **not** pickaxe-mineable. Chop them with an [[Axe]] (which can take several wood cards at once and yields more per card), or **play a single Wood block by hand** as a one-card hand for **+1 Wood**.

## Tool tiers (mining level)
Each ore needs a pickaxe of at least a minimum **mining level** — faithful to Minecraft. A too-weak pickaxe flashes "Need a better pickaxe" and leaves the block intact.

| Pickaxe material | Mining level | Can break |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden | 0 | Cobblestone, Coal, Sand |
| Stone | 1 | + Raw Iron, Lapis Lazuli |
| Iron | 2 | + Raw Gold, Redstone, Diamond, Emerald |
| **Golden** | **0** | only the wood-tier ores (the famous gold-pickaxe quirk: fast, but mines no better than wood) |
| Diamond | 3 | + Netherite, [[Obsidian]] |
| Netherite | 4 | everything |

So [[Netherite]] and [[Obsidian]] blocks both need a **Diamond pickaxe (level 3) or better**. [[Iron]], [[Gold]] and [[Glass]] are *not* mined — they're smelt-only (see [[Smelting]]); you mine **Raw Iron**, **Raw Gold** and **Sand** instead.

## Fortune
A pickaxe with the **Fortune** enchant ([[Enchanting]]) adds a random bonus to each yield (up to +1 / +2 / +3 by tier), so a single block can drop extra ore.
