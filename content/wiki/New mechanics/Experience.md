*Mechanic*

![[xp_bar.png|150]]

An **XP bar** that fills as you play, using Minecraft's real leveling curve. XP and level are tracked per-run (they start at 0 and reset each run).

## Earning XP
| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Beat a blind | (Small 2 / Big 3 / Boss 6) **× current ante** |
| Score chips | 1 XP per 100 chips scored |
| Buy a joker or consumable | 3 |
| Redeem a voucher | 10 |
| Sell a joker or consumable | 2 |
| Complete a craft | 5 |

> XP awards are placeholder balance and may be tuned.

> **Planned XP sources:** the [[Lapis]] enhancement (+3 on play), the [[Soul Seal]] (+5 on play), and the [[XP Tag]] (≈ a level on skip) will also grant XP.

## Levels
Each level costs more, matching Minecraft exactly — XP needed to go from level **L → L+1**:
- **L ≤ 15:** `2L + 7`
- **16 ≤ L ≤ 30:** `5L − 38`
- **L ≥ 31:** `9L − 158`

Leveling up plays a chime and the bar rolls over.

## Spending XP
Levels power the **[[Enchanting]]** mechanic: applying an [[Enchanting Books|enchanting book]] to a [[Tools|tool]] **spends levels** (the cost scales with the book's tier and the tool's material). Earn levels by playing; spend them to make your tools stronger and more durable. See [[Enchanting]].

> *(Planned)* Card **[[Editions]]** are applied through this same system — a card [[Enchanting Books|Enchanting Book]] used on a **playing card** also spends levels.
