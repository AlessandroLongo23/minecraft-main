*Mechanic*

![[xp_bar.png|150]]

An **XP bar** that fills as you play, using Minecraft's real leveling curve. XP and level are tracked per-run (they start at 0 and reset each run).

## Earning XP
XP is **event-based** (not tied to raw score — that exploded on Balatro's super-exponential curve). The spine of progression is beating blinds, scaled by **ante²** so leveling keeps pace with the difficulty curve.

| Action | XP |
| --- | --- |
| Beat a blind | (Night/Small 4 / Cave/Big 7 / Boss 14) **× ante²** |
| Buy a joker or consumable | 3 |
| Redeem a voucher | 10 |
| Sell a joker or consumable | 2 |
| Complete a craft | 5 |

Baseline level by the end of each ante (before craft/buy bonuses) is roughly **2, 8, 15, 23, 29, 36, 42, 49**.

> XP awards are placeholder balance and may be tuned.

## Levels
Each level costs more, matching Minecraft exactly — XP needed to go from level **L → L+1**:
- **L ≤ 15:** `2L + 7`
- **16 ≤ L ≤ 30:** `5L − 38`
- **L ≥ 31:** `9L − 158`

Leveling up plays a chime and the bar rolls over.

## Spending XP
Levels power **[[Enchanting]]**: at the **Enchanting Table** station you spend levels (plus [[Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]] and a Book) to **generate** an [[Enchanting Books|enchanting book]] — the higher the book tier, the more levels it requires (Lv 7 / 18 / 30 for tier I / II / III). *Applying* a finished book to a tool (at the [[Anvil]]) or a card is then **free**. Earn levels by playing; spend them to forge stronger enchants. See [[Enchanting]].
