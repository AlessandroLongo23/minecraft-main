*Mechanic*

The **Composter** is a [[The Base|Base]] station that turns otherwise-useless **organic** items into [[Bone Meal]] — giving flowers and farm plants a purpose.

## Building the Composter
Craft it once at the [[Crafting]] table from **7 [[Wood]]** (the open-topped bin shape — a U with a hollow top). Crafting it unlocks the Composter in [[The Base]].

## How it works
Open the Composter to see the organic items you own. **Click an item** to drop one into the bin. Every **7 items composted** yields **1 [[Bone Meal]]** consumable.

When the bin fills, a Bone Meal is collected automatically if you have a free consumable slot; otherwise a **Collect Bone Meal** button appears so you can claim it manually. The Composter won't accept more input while it's full and can't empty, so organics are never wasted.

## What counts as organic
Two groups feed the Composter, and both exist *only* to be composted:

- **Flowers** — Dandelion, Poppy, Cornflower, Oxeye Daisy.
- **Farm plants** — Sugar Cane, Carrot, Melon Slice, Brown Mushroom.

Flowers drop only from the overworld organic roll on a blind win, so the Composter is their single use.
