*Mechanic*

BalaCraft's world is split into three **dimensions** — the **Overworld**, the **Nether**, and **The End**. Each ante is themed by one [[Biomes|biome]], and biomes belong to a dimension, so where you are decides which biomes, bosses and drops you'll see.

## Picking a biome each ante
You always start in the **Overworld** (Plains). After every **boss cash-out**, before the shop builds, you're offered **three biomes** from your current dimension (your current biome is excluded) — see [[Biomes]]. Your pick themes the next ante: its background tint, ore bias, exclusive bosses, and a one-time arrival effect.

## Travelling to the Nether
The Nether is reached through a **Ruined Portal**. Ruined Portals are a special structure blind that can appear on any overworld Night/Cave slot (see [[New Bosses]] for the structure reskins).

While playing a Ruined Portal blind, **Use [[Flint and Steel]]** while you hold at least **1 [[Obsidian]]**:
- It spends **1 Obsidian** to light the portal,
- instantly wins the blind,
- warps you to a random **Nether** biome,
- and rerolls this ante's boss into a Nether boss.

Craft Flint and Steel from [[Iron]] + Flint (so it's gated behind a Furnace), and mine [[Obsidian]] with a Diamond pickaxe ([[Mining]]).

## Travelling to The End
The End is reached by following the **Eye of Ender trail** over **three consecutive antes**:

1. Craft and **Use an [[Eye of Ender]]** (1 Ender Pearl + 1 Blaze Powder). This *arms* the next biome selection, which shows a directional marker.
2. Follow the marked option each ante for three antes in a row: a random biome → the **Stronghold** → **The End**.

You must use an Eye **every ante** to keep the chain going — skip an ante and the trail resets to the start. The Stronghold is a ritual-only waypoint that never appears in a normal biome roll.

> The old portal **vouchers** that used to switch dimensions have been removed — the Ruined Portal and the Eye-of-Ender trail are now the only routes.

## Structure blinds
The Overworld Night/Cave (non-boss) blinds can be re-themed each ante as Minecraft **structures** — such as the Desert Temple, Witch Hut, Igloo, Woodland Mansion, Pillager Outpost, Shipwreck, Mineshaft, Dungeon and Jungle Temple, plus the special **Ruined Portal**. Structures are tied to fitting biomes (an Igloo in [[Snowy Taiga]], a Witch Hut in [[Swamp]], …); the Ruined Portal can surface anywhere in the Overworld. See [[New Bosses]] for the blind reskins.

## Dimension flavour
- **Overworld** — 8 biomes (Plains, Forest, Desert, Snowy Taiga, Jungle, Swamp, Savanna, Badlands), plus the ritual-only Stronghold. Home of farm/flower drops and the per-biome [[New Decks|starting decks]].
- **Nether** — fiery biomes (Nether Wastes, Crimson Forest, Soul Sand Valley, Basalt Deltas) that favor Netherite, with the [[New Decks|Nether]] deck and Nether bosses.
- **The End** — a single biome, **The End**, watched by a sentinel; home of the [[New Decks|End]] deck.
