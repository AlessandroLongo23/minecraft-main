*Mechanic*

The **Anvil** is a [[The Base|Base]] station that maintains and upgrades your [[Tools]]. It's a tabbed workshop: forge [[Sheets]], merge tools, apply [[Enchanting Books]], and upgrade to Netherite.

## Building the Anvil
Craft it once at the [[Crafting]] table from **5 [[Iron]]** (an anvil silhouette). Because Iron is **smelt-only**, the Anvil is naturally gated behind owning a **Furnace** first — thematically faithful (you need a furnace to make iron, and iron to make an anvil). Crafting it unlocks the Anvil in [[The Base]].

## Tabs
The Anvil opens to a tab bar. The tools and books it works on are the ones in your equipped Minecraft consumable slots, so swap a tool in from your inventory before using a tab.

### Forge Sheets
Drag **2 of one ore** into the two input slots, then **Forge** to produce that ore's [[Sheets|Sheet]] (a card-modifier consumable). Both slots must hold the same forgeable ore.

### Combine Tools
Select **two tools of the same type and material** (e.g. two Iron Pickaxes) and **Combine**:
- The first-selected tool **survives** and takes the **highest tier of each enchant** from both.
- Their remaining durability is **pooled** (capped at the survivor's max — not a free full repair).
- The other tool is consumed.

This is how you stack enchantments and bank spare durability.

### Enchant
Pick a tool and an [[Enchanting Books|Enchant Book]], then **Apply**. **Applying is free** — no level or Lapis cost (those are paid earlier, when the book is generated at the [[Enchanting]] Table). The book is consumed and its enchant lands on the tool.

### Upgrade
The Smithing-Table stand-in. Select a **Diamond tool**, spend **1 [[Netherite]]**, and **Upgrade** to its Netherite counterpart. Faithful to Minecraft: netherite gear isn't crafted, it's upgraded — and the upgrade **carries over the diamond tool's enchants and its durability damage** (a worn, enchanted diamond becomes a worn, enchanted netherite).
