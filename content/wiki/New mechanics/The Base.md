*Mechanic*

**Your Base** is the home screen for everything you build — your crafting **stations** and your **inventory**. Open it from the button under the resource hotbar.

## Stations
Each station is a **one-time craft** at the [[Crafting]] table (its recipe's output is a `station`). Until you craft it, the Base shows it greyed with a "(craft it)" label; once built, it stays unlocked for the run and its button opens its overlay (or applies its passive buff).

| Station | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Crafting Table** | — (always open) | The 3×3 [[Crafting]] grid. |
| **Furnace** | 8 [[Cobblestone]] | [[Smelting|Smelt]] raw ores into Iron / Gold / Glass. |
| **[[Anvil]]** | 5 [[Iron]] | Forge Sheets, combine tools, apply enchants, upgrade to Netherite. |
| **Chest** | 8 [[Wood]] | Passive: expands inventory capacity (see below). |
| **[[Composter]]** | 7 [[Wood]] | Turn organics into [[Bone Meal]]. |
| **[[Brewing Stand]]** | 1 Blaze Rod + 3 [[Cobblestone]] | Brew [[Potions]]. |
| **Enchanting Table** | 1 Book + 2 [[Diamond]] + 4 [[Obsidian]] | Generate [[Enchanting Books]] (see [[Enchanting]]). |

Iron is smelt-only, so the **Anvil** is effectively gated behind a **Furnace**. The Enchanting Table needs Obsidian and a Book, so it comes online late.

## Inventory & capacity
The Minecraft inventory has **real capacity** — a fixed number of slots:
- Base capacity is **18 slots**.
- Building the **Chest** adds **+9 slots**.
- Resources **auto-stack up to 16 per slot**; a half-full stack still uses one slot.
- Every stored Minecraft consumable (a tool, food, book, sheet or potion) takes **one slot**.

If something wouldn't fit, the action that would produce it is blocked rather than discarding the overflow — this is why [[Smelting]] and the [[Composter]] check for room first.

## Consumable slots
There are **two separate consumable areas** sharing the on-screen hotbar spot:
- **Vanilla consumables** — your normal Tarot / Planet / Spectral slots, which still grow with vouchers.
- **Minecraft consumables** — a dedicated **2-slot** area for BalaCraft tools, food, [[Enchanting Books|books]], [[Sheets]] and [[Potions]]. These two equipped slots are **separate** and do **not** count against inventory capacity.

A toggle flips which area is shown. Store an item back into the inventory to free a Minecraft slot, then equip another.
