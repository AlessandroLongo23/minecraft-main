*Mechanic*

**Villager Trading** is a persistent section of the **shop** where you spend ![[icon_emerald.png|20]] [[Emerald|emeralds]] on goods the normal shop doesn't sell. Where every other ore is a crafting material, Emerald is *money*: you spend it here.

## How it works
Each shop visit spawns **one villager profession** (chosen randomly per shop), and shows a small set of **trade offers** (3) from that profession's pool. Each offer has an **emerald price** and a **Trade** button — green when you can afford it, greyed when you can't (or, for a card, when you have no free consumable slot). Buying spends the emeralds, grants the item, and marks that offer **Sold** for the visit.

- A **profession** is fixed for the shop visit; its pool decides what's on offer.
- The villager has its **own reroll**, paid in **Emeralds** (starts at 1, **+1 each reroll**, resets per shop). It re-rolls the *trades* but keeps the *profession* — and it's independent of the normal $ shop reroll.
- Buying an offer **sells it out** until the next shop.
- The panel toggles in over the shop footprint and only shows while you're in the shop.

## The professions
| Profession | Sells | Emerald cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Toolsmith** | Any [[Tools\|tool]] (Sword / Pickaxe / Shovel / Axe), Wooden→Netherite | 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 8 / 10 by material |
| **Librarian** | [[Enchanting Books]] (Sharpness/Durability/Fortune I–III) + a [[Torch]] | books 5 / 8 / 12 by tier; Torch 2 |
| **Mason** | Ore/resource **bundles** (a random ore of a tier) | 5× tier-1 = 3, 3× tier-2 = 4, 2× tier-3 = 6, 1× tier-4 = 8 |
| **Farmer** | [[Food]] consumables | 1 / 1 / 2 by food tier |

The offer mix within a profession is weighted by sub-category (tool type, enchant type, bundle tier) so you don't get three of the same thing.

## Getting emeralds to spend
[[Emerald]] is a gathered ore: it drops from boss blinds at its tier and appears in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]. See [[Resource Drops]].
