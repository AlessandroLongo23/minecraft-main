## Effect
Eat to restore **4 hunger points** — the best of the tier-1 foods, for a dollar more. Eating past full [[Hunger]] banks the overflow as saturation, which converts to [[Health|hearts]]. Can't be eaten when both hunger and saturation are full.

## Where to get it
Shop, any [[Booster Packs|Food Pack]] (tier-1, most common), or the Farmer in [[Villager Trading]].
