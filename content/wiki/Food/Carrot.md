## Effect
Eat to restore **3 hunger points**. Cheap, common, and a solid early-run snack. Eating past full [[Hunger]] banks the overflow as saturation, which converts to [[Health|hearts]]. Can't be eaten when both hunger and saturation are full.

## Where to get it
Shop, any [[Booster Packs|Food Pack]] (tier-1, most common), or the Farmer in [[Villager Trading]].
