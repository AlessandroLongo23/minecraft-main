Food cards restore your [[Hunger]]. Unlike [[Resources]] and [[Tools]], food **does appear in the shop** (about half as often as a Tarot), in dedicated **[[Booster Packs|Food Packs]]**, and from the Farmer in [[Villager Trading]].

Hunger is measured in points where **2 points = 1 drumstick** on the bar (max 20 = 10 drumsticks). Each blind drains hunger; letting it hit zero **starves** you for a heart of [[Health|damage]] per blind. You **can't eat when both hunger and saturation are full** (so a card is never wasted).

**Over-eating banks [[Health|saturation]].** Eating past full hunger stores the overflow as **saturation** (gold drumsticks, capped at the bar's max). Each blind, banked saturation is spent to **heal your hearts** — 2 saturation → 1 heart — but only while you're below full HP. So a surplus snack now turns into healing later. See [[Health]] and [[Hunger]].

## Where you get food
- **Shop** — foods roll like a minor consumable (shop rate ≈ half a Tarot's).
- **[[Booster Packs|Food Packs]]** — Food Pack / Jumbo / Mega, tier-weighted 4 : 2 : 1 (so common foods show up far more than Cooked Porkchop).
- **[[Villager Trading]]** — the Farmer sells foods for Emeralds.

See [[Hunger]] for how eating, saturation and starvation work, and [[Health]] for hearts.
