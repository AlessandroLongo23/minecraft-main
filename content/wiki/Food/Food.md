Food cards restore your [[Hunger]]. Unlike [[Resources]] and [[Tools]], food **does appear in the shop** (about half as often as a Tarot) and in dedicated **[[Booster Packs|Food Packs]]**.

Hunger is measured in points where **2 points = 1 drumstick** on the bar (max 20 = 10 drumsticks). Keeping your hunger high passively heals your [[Health|hearts]]; letting it hit zero starves you. You **can't eat at full hunger** (it won't waste the card).

## Where you get food
- **Shop** — foods roll like a minor consumable (shop rate ≈ half a Tarot's).
- **[[Booster Packs|Food Packs]]** — Food Pack / Jumbo / Mega, tier-weighted 4 : 2 : 1 (so common foods show up far more than Cooked Porkchop).

See [[Hunger]] for how eating, well-fed regen and starvation work.
