Eat to restore **8 hunger points** — the most filling food in the game (nearly half the bar). Can't be eaten at full [[Hunger]].

## Where to get it
Shop, or any [[Booster Packs|Food Pack]] (tier-3, rarest — ~1 in 17 per pack slot).
