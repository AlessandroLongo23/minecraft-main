*Enchanting Book — 3 tiers · dual-target (any [[Tools|tool]] **or** playing card)*

![[res_book.png|105]]

## Effect
Durability is **dual-target**:
- On any **[[Tools|tool]]**: **multiplies its max Uses** (rounded up) and grants the extra uses immediately. One use is spent per blind. Base Uses by material are Wood 1 / Cobblestone 2 / Iron 3 / Gold 3 / Diamond 4 / Netherite 5 — so a Diamond tool (4) with Durability III (×2) lasts **8 blinds**.
- On a **playing card**: applies the **[[Editions/Unbreaking|Unbreaking edition]]** — the card **can't be debuffed by bosses** (and Glass won't break). Flat, the same at any book tier.

## Getting & applying
- **Get it** at the [[Enchanting Table]] (tier-`n` offer = `n` [[Experience|levels]] + `n` [[Resources/Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]] + 1 [[Resources/Book|Book]]), or buy it in the shop / from an [[Enchanting Pack]].
- **To a tool:** select the tool + this book at the **[[Anvil]]** → **Enchant** (**free**; one Durability per tool, a higher tier upgrades it).
- **To a card:** highlight a card in hand + this book during a blind → **Enchant Card** to apply the [[Editions/Unbreaking|Unbreaking edition]] (also free).
