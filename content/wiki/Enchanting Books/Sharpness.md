*Enchanting Book — 3 tiers · dual-target ([[Sword]] **or** playing card)*

![[res_book.png|105]]

## Effect
Sharpness is **dual-target** — the same book enchants either a tool or a playing card:
- On a **[[Sword]]**: **multiplies its ×Mult** by the book's tier (on top of the material value). A Diamond Sword (×8) with Sharpness III becomes **×14**.
- On a **playing card**: applies the **[[Editions/Sharpness|Sharpness edition]]** — **+Mult when the card scores, doubled against a boss (mob) blind**. The card bonus scales with the book's tier.

## Getting & applying
- **Get it** at the [[Enchanting Table]] (tier-`n` offer = `n` [[Experience|levels]] + `n` [[Resources/Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]] + 1 [[Resources/Book|Book]]), or buy it in the shop / from an [[Enchanting Pack]].
- **To a [[Sword]]:** select the sword + this book at the **[[Anvil]]** → **Enchant**. **Free** — the cost was paid making the book. A higher tier upgrades an existing Sharpness.
- **To a card:** during a blind, highlight a card in hand + this book → **Enchant Card** to apply the [[Editions/Sharpness|Sharpness edition]] (also free).
