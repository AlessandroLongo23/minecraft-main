*Enchanting Book — 3 tiers · dual-target ([[Pickaxe]]/[[Shovel]] **or** playing card)*

![[res_book.png|105]]

## Effect
Fortune is **dual-target**:
- On a **[[Pickaxe]]**: adds a **random 0…tier bonus** to the ore it pulls each use (so a Fortune III pickaxe rolls +0 to +3 extra ore).
- On a **[[Shovel]]**: the shovel is a gamble that pays only on a hit, and Fortune raises the **find chance** instead of the loot — base 50%, **+15% per tier** (50% → 65% → 80% → 95%).
- On a **playing card**: applies the **[[Editions/Lucky|Lucky edition]]** — the card **drops Emeralds when it scores** (averaging 0.5 / 1.5 / 2.5 Emeralds by book tier).

## Getting & applying
- **Get it** at the [[Enchanting Table]] (tier-`n` offer = `n` [[Experience|levels]] + `n` [[Resources/Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]] + 1 [[Resources/Book|Book]]), or buy it in the shop / from an [[Enchanting Pack]].
- **To a tool:** select a [[Pickaxe]] or [[Shovel]] + this book at the **[[Anvil]]** → **Enchant** (**free**; one Fortune per tool, a higher tier upgrades it).
- **To a card:** highlight a card in hand + this book during a blind → **Enchant Card** to apply the [[Editions/Lucky|Lucky edition]] (also free).
