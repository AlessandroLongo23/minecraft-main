## Effect
Applied to a **[[Pickaxe]]** or **[[Shovel]]**, Fortune adds a **random 0…tier bonus** to that tool's payout each time you use it — extra ore for the Pickaxe, extra $ for the Shovel.

## Tiers
| Tier | Bonus loot (random) | Shop $ | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fortune I | +0 to +1 | $4 | `c_balacraft_enchant_fortune_1` |
| Fortune II | +0 to +2 | $7 | `c_balacraft_enchant_fortune_2` |
| Fortune III | +0 to +3 | $10 | `c_balacraft_enchant_fortune_3` |

## Applying
**Dual-target.** Select a [[Pickaxe]] or [[Shovel]] + this book → **Enchant** (costs `book tier + material tier − 1` [[Experience|levels]]; one per tool, higher tier upgrades). Or select a **card in hand** + this book → **Enchant Card** to apply the [[Editions/Lucky\|Lucky edition]] — the card drops Emeralds on score (avg 0.5 / 1.5 / 2.5 by tier; costs `book tier + 1` levels). See [[Enchanting]].
