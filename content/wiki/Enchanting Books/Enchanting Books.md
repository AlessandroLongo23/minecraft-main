Implemented: 9 (3 types × 3 tiers)

*Dual-target [[Enchanting|enchantments]] — each book enchants a [[Tools|tool]] **or** a playing card.*

![[res_book.png|100]]

**Enchanting Books** are the consumables that carry [[Enchanting|enchantments]]. There are **three types** — [[Enchanting Books/Sharpness|Sharpness]], [[Durability]] and [[Fortune]] — each in **three tiers** (I / II / III).

A book isn't "used" on its own. You **select a target + a book**, then click **Enchant**: on a [[Tools|tool]] this happens at the **[[Anvil]]**, on a playing card it happens via the in-blind **Enchant Card** flow. **Applying is free** — you already paid when you got the book.

| Book | Applies to | Tool effect (by tier) | Card effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Enchanting Books/Sharpness\|Sharpness]] | Sword **or** card | ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×1.75 sword ×Mult | [[Editions/Sharpness\|Sharpness edition]] (+Mult) |
| [[Durability]] | Any tool **or** card | ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×2 max Uses | [[Editions/Unbreaking\|Unbreaking edition]] |
| [[Fortune]] | Pickaxe / Shovel **or** card | up to +1 / +2 / +3 loot | [[Editions/Lucky\|Lucky edition]] (drops Emeralds) |

## Where books come from
- **Generated at the [[Enchanting Table]]** (the main source): each of the three offered tiers costs **that many [[Experience|levels]] + that much [[Resources/Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]] + 1 [[Resources/Book|Book]]**, and needs an [[Experience|XP level]] of at least **7 / 18 / 30** for tier I / II / III.
- **Bought in the shop** (for $) or opened from an **[[Enchanting Pack]]** — both unlocked by the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher.

## Rules
- A tool holds **one of each type**; applying a higher tier upgrades it (you can't downgrade). Different types stack.
- **Tier III** books only appear in shops/packs once you own the [[Sorcerer's Tome]] voucher (which also flips Enchanting Packs toward higher tiers).
- Each book is a tinted enchanted-book card — Sharpness orange, Durability cyan, Fortune green — with its tier (I/II/III) shown.
- **All three are dual-target.** A card holds one [[Editions|edition]] at a time: Sharpness → [[Editions/Sharpness\|+Mult]], Durability → [[Editions/Unbreaking\|Unbreaking]], Fortune → [[Editions/Lucky\|Lucky]].
