Implemented: 9 (3 types × 3 tiers)

---
**Enchanting Books** apply [[Enchanting|enchantments]] to your [[Tools]]. They're bought in the shop or opened from an [[Enchanting Pack]] — both unlocked by the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher. A book isn't "used" on its own: **select a tool + a book**, then click **Enchant** (it costs [[Experience|levels]], not money).

| Book | Applies to | Effect (by tier) | Shop $ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Enchanting Books/Sharpness\|Sharpness]] | Sword **or** card | tool ×1.25/1.5/1.75 · card [[Editions/Sharpness\|+Mult edition]] | $4 / $7 / $10 |
| [[Durability]] | All tools **or** card | ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×2 Uses · card [[Editions/Unbreaking\|Unbreaking edition]] | $4 / $7 / $10 |
| [[Fortune]] | Pickaxe, Shovel **or** card | up to +1 / +2 / +3 loot · card [[Editions/Lucky\|Lucky edition]] | $4 / $7 / $10 |

- A tool holds **one of each type**; a higher tier upgrades it.
- **Tier III** books only appear once you own the [[Sorcerer's Tome]] voucher.
- Books reuse ore-card art as a placeholder for now.

> **All three books are dual-target** — each enchants a tool *or* a playing card. Sharpness → [[Editions/Sharpness\|+Mult edition]]; Durability → [[Editions/Unbreaking\|Unbreaking edition]]; Fortune → [[Editions/Lucky\|Lucky edition]]. A card holds one [[Editions|edition]] at a time.
