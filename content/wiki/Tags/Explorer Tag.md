*Tag — earned by skipping a blind*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Reward | +1 choice at the next biome select |
| Lane | [[Biomes]] |
| Internal key | `tag_balacraft_explorer` |

## Effect
Earned by skipping a blind. At the **next biome-select screen** (after the next boss) you get **one extra
[[Biomes|biome]] choice** — 4 options instead of 3 — improving your odds of landing the biome you want for
its resource bias, exclusive boss, or arrival bonus.

## Notes
- Only appears when the [[Biomes]] system is enabled.
