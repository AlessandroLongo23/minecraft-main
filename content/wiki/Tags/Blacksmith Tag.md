## Effect
Earned by skipping a blind. Grants a **free random [[Tools|Tool]]** — handed to you directly, skipping the
[[Crafting]] table. The tool's **material tier is capped by the current ante** (min of 5 and the ante), so
you get gear roughly appropriate to how deep you are. A shortcut to a tool when you can't spare the ore.

## Notes
- Only appears when the [[Resources]] system is enabled.
- The Blacksmith Tag *gives* a tool; the [[Enchanting Books/Sharpness|Sharpness]] Enchanting Book
  *enchants* one. Different things.
