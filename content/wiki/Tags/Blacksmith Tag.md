## Effect
Earned by skipping a blind. Grants a **free random [[Tools|Tool]]** (Sword / Pickaxe / Shovel) at the
ante's unlocked material tier — handed to you directly, skipping the [[Crafting]] table. A shortcut to
gear when you can't spare the ore.

## Notes
- The Blacksmith Tag *gives* a tool; the [[Enchanting Books/Sharpness\|Sharpness]] Enchanting Book *enchants* one. Different things.
