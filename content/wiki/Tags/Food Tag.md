*Tag — earned by skipping a blind*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Reward | a free [[Booster Packs\|Food Pack]] |
| Lane | New packs |
| Internal key | `tag_balacraft_food` |

## Effect
Earned by skipping a blind. When the tag triggers, a free **[[Booster Packs|Food Pack]]** **opens right
there on the skip screen** (like vanilla pack tags) — [[Food]] to manage your [[Hunger]]. The food
counterpart to the [[Loot Tag]].

## Notes
- Only appears when the [[Food]] system is enabled.
