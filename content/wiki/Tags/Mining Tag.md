## Effect
Earned by skipping a blind. Instantly gain **3 of a random ore**, rolled from your current
[[Biomes|biome]] at the ante's unlocked tier — the same roll as blind drops and the [[Ore Seal]]. A quick
top-up of crafting materials without playing the blind.

## Notes
- Only appears when the [[Resources]] system is enabled.
