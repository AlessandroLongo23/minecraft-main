A Food Pack gives you **[[Food]]** to keep your [[Hunger]] topped up. Pick from the cards shown; each pick adds that food card to your consumables.

## Sizes
| Pack | Cost | Shop weight | Shows | Pick | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Food Pack | $4 | 1 | up to 3 | 1 | `p_balacraft_food_pack` |
| Jumbo Food Pack | $6 | 0.5 | up to 5 | 1 | `p_balacraft_food_pack_jumbo` |
| Mega Food Pack | $8 | 0.25 | up to 5 | 2 | `p_balacraft_food_pack_mega` |

## Contents
Any of the six [[Food|foods]] can appear. Sampling is tier-weighted **4 : 2 : 1**:
- Tier 1 (each ≈23.5%/slot): [[Potato]], [[Carrot]], [[Apple]]
- Tier 2 (each ≈11.8%/slot): [[Baked Potato]], [[Cooked Chicken]]
- Tier 3 (≈5.9%/slot): [[Cooked Porkchop]]

Cards within one pack don't repeat.
