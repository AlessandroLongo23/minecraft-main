A Potion Pack lets you pick **1 of up to 3 [[Potions]]**. The potions are plain (un-modified) brews — no Potency / Duration / Lingering upgrades. Each pick adds that potion to your consumables.

The pack is a **secondary** source of potions: the primary way to get them is to brew your own at the **[[Brewing Stand]]** (where you also choose the upgrades).

## Details
| Pack | Cost | Shop weight | Shows | Pick | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Potion Pack | $4 | 1 | up to 3 | 1 | `p_balacraft_potion_pack` |

Unlike the [[Loot Chest]], [[Food Pack]] and [[Toolbox]], the Potion Pack has **no Jumbo / Mega variant** — there's only the one size.

## Contents
Any of the eight [[Potions]] can appear (the six drinkables plus the two Splash throwables). Sampling is **distinct** (no repeats within a pack) and tier-weighted **4 : 2 : 1**, so common potions show up far more often than rare ones:
- Tier 1: [[Potions/Swiftness|Swiftness]], [[Potions/Healing|Healing]], [[Potions/Night Vision|Night Vision]]
- Tier 2: [[Potions/Strength|Strength]], [[Potions/Instant Health|Instant Health]], [[Potions/Poison|Poison]]
- Tier 3: [[Potions/Invisibility|Invisibility]], [[Potions/Harming|Harming]]

> Tip: use a [[Torch]] in the shop to **peek a pack's contents before buying**.
