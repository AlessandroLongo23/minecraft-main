A Toolbox gives you **[[Tools]]** straight from the shop — no [[Crafting]] required. Pick from the cards shown; each pick adds that tool (or the [[Torch]]) to your consumables. Just like a Minecraft loot chest, a tool can occasionally turn up **already enchanted**, even before you own an [[Enchanting Table]].

## Sizes
| Pack | Cost | Shop weight | Shows | Pick | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Toolbox | $6 | 0.5 | up to 3 | 1 | `p_balacraft_tool_pack` |
| Jumbo Toolbox | $8 | 0.35 | up to 5 | 1 | `p_balacraft_tool_pack_jumbo` |
| Mega Toolbox | $10 | 0.2 | up to 5 | 2 | `p_balacraft_tool_pack_mega` |

## Contents
All 18 [[Tools]] (Sword / Pickaxe / Shovel × Wood → Netherite) plus the [[Torch]] can appear. Which material tiers are available **scales with the ante**, and low tiers are always the most common:

| Ante | Highest material |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Stone |
| 3–4 | Iron |
| 5 | Gold |
| 6–7 | Diamond |
| 8+ | Netherite |

Cards within one pack don't repeat. The Torch can show up at any ante and is never enchanted.

## Enchantments
When the [[Enchanting Books|enchanting system]] is active, each tool slot has a small chance to be **pre-enchanted** — no voucher needed:
- **Chance** rises with the ante: ~5% at ante 1, up to a 25% cap.
- **Type** is always valid for the tool: [[Sharpness]] or [[Durability]] on a Sword; [[Fortune]] or [[Durability]] on a Pickaxe/Shovel.
- **Tier** is almost always **I**; **II** can appear from ante 4 and **III** from ante 7.
