Implemented: 10 (3 lines × 3 sizes + Enchanting Pack)

BalaCraft adds new booster packs that roll in the shop alongside the vanilla packs:

- **[[Loot Chest]]** — gives you [[Resources|ores]] for [[Crafting]].
- **[[Food Pack]]** — gives you [[Food]] to manage [[Hunger]].
- **[[Enchanting Pack]]** — gives you [[Enchanting Books]] (only after the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher).
- **[[Toolbox]]** — gives you [[Tools]] (incl. the [[Torch]]) straight from the shop, occasionally **pre-enchanted**. Comes in three sizes, but unlike the packs below it is **tuned differently** — lower base weights and **ante-scaled** contents (see [[Toolbox]]).

> **Planned:** the [[Loot Tag]] and [[Food Tag]] (skip-blind [[Tags]]) will grant a free Loot Chest / Food Pack in the next shop.

The Loot Chest and Food Pack each come in three sizes — normal, **Jumbo**, **Mega** — tuned identically:

| Size | Cost | Shop weight | Cards shown | Picks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | $4 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| Jumbo | $6 | 0.5 | 5 | 1 |
| Mega | $8 | 0.25 | 5 | 2 |

## How the contents are rolled
Both lines sample **without replacement** (every card in a pack is distinct), **tier-weighted 4 : 2 : 1** for tiers 1 : 2 : 3 — so common items appear far more often than rare ones. There's **no ante scaling**; the weights are fixed.

- Per slot, each tier-1 item ≈ 4/17 (≈23.5%), each tier-2 ≈ 2/17 (≈11.8%), the single tier-3 ≈ 1/17 (≈5.9%).
- A **Mega** pack (5 of 6 possible items) always shows 5 of the 6 — one item is cut each time, weighting decides which.

> Tip: use a [[Torch]] in the shop to **peek a pack's contents before buying**.
