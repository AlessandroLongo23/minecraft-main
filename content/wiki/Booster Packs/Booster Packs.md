---
BalaCraft adds new booster packs that roll in the shop alongside the vanilla packs:

- **[[Loot Chest]]** — gives you [[Resources|ores]] for [[Crafting]].
- **[[Food Pack]]** — gives you [[Food]] to manage [[Hunger]].
- **[[Potion Pack]]** — gives you [[Potions]] (the secondary source; brewing at the [[Brewing Stand]] is the primary one).
- **[[Enchanting Pack]]** — gives you [[Enchanting Books]] (only after the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher).
- **[[Toolbox]]** — gives you [[Tools]] (incl. the [[Torch]]) straight from the shop, occasionally **pre-enchanted**. Comes in three sizes, but unlike the packs below it is **tuned differently** — lower base weights and **ante-scaled** contents (see [[Toolbox]]).

> **Planned:** the [[Loot Tag]] and [[Food Tag]] (skip-blind [[Tags]]) will grant a free Loot Chest / Food Pack in the next shop.

The Loot Chest and Food Pack each come in three sizes — normal, **Jumbo**, **Mega** — tuned identically. The Potion Pack and Enchanting Pack are **single-size only**:

| Size | Cost | Shop weight | Cards shown | Picks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | $4 | 1 | 3 | 1 |
| Jumbo | $6 | 0.5 | 5 | 1 |
| Mega | $8 | 0.25 | 5 | 2 |

(The single-size [[Potion Pack]] matches the Normal row: $4, weight 1, shows up to 3, pick 1. The [[Enchanting Pack]] is $6, weight 0.6, shows up to 3, pick 1.)

## How the contents are rolled
The resource, food and potion lines all sample **without replacement** (every card in a pack is distinct), **tier-weighted 4 : 2 : 1** for tiers 1 : 2 : 3 — so common items appear far more often than rare ones. There's **no ante scaling** on these; the weights are fixed. (The [[Toolbox]] and [[Enchanting Pack]] are weighted differently — see their pages.)

- Per slot in a 6-item, 3-tier pool, each tier-1 item ≈ 4/17 (≈23.5%), each tier-2 ≈ 2/17 (≈11.8%), the single tier-3 ≈ 1/17 (≈5.9%).
- A **Mega** pack shows 5 of the 6 possible items — one item is cut each time, weighting decides which.

> Tip: use a [[Torch]] in the shop to **peek a pack's contents before buying**.
