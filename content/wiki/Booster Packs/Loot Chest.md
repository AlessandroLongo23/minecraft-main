A Loot Chest gives you **[[Resources|ores]]** to spend on [[Crafting]]. Pick from the cards shown; each pick adds that ore to your hotbar.

## Contents
Only **ore-class gathered resources** appear — the mineable blocks and raw drops. Mob drops (String, Bone, …), refined goods (Iron, Gold, Glass), "special" items (flowers, Nether/End materials) and crafted intermediates like [[Sticks]] **never** show up here. Sampling is tier-weighted **4 : 2 : 1 : 1** for tiers 1 : 2 : 3 : 4:
- **Tier 1** (most common): [[Wood]], [[Cobblestone]], [[Coal]], [[Sand]]
- **Tier 2**: [[Emerald]], [[Raw Iron]], [[Raw Gold]]
- **Tier 3**: [[Diamond]], [[Redstone]], [[Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]]
- **Tier 4** (rarest): [[Netherite]]

Cards within one pack don't repeat, and bigger packs (more cards shown) improve the odds of surfacing a rare ore like Netherite.
