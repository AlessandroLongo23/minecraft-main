A Loot Chest gives you **[[Resources|ores]]** to spend on [[Crafting]]. Pick from the cards shown; each pick adds that ore to your hotbar.

## Sizes
| Pack | Cost | Shop weight | Shows | Pick | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Loot Chest | $4 | 1 | up to 3 | 1 | `p_balacraft_resource_pack` |
| Jumbo Loot Chest | $6 | 0.5 | up to 5 | 1 | `p_balacraft_resource_pack_jumbo` |
| Mega Loot Chest | $8 | 0.25 | up to 5 | 2 | `p_balacraft_resource_pack_mega` |

## Contents
Only **gathered ores** appear (Wood, Cobblestone, Coal, Iron, Gold, Diamond) — [[Sticks]] never show. Sampling is tier-weighted **4 : 2 : 1**:
- Tier 1 (each ≈23.5%/slot): [[Wood]], [[Cobblestone]], [[Coal]]
- Tier 2 (each ≈11.8%/slot): [[Iron]], [[Gold]]
- Tier 3 (≈5.9%/slot): [[Diamond]]

Cards within one pack don't repeat.
