An Enchanting Pack lets you pick **1 of up to 3 [[Enchanting Books]]**. It only rolls in the shop once you own the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher.

## Details
| Pack | Cost | Shop weight | Shows | Pick | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enchanting Pack | $6 | 0.6 | up to 3 | 1 | `p_balacraft_enchant_pack` |

## Contents
Samples **distinct** books, tier-weighted. By default it favours **low tiers** (4 : 2 : 1 for tiers I : II : III); once you own the [[Sorcerer's Tome]] voucher the weights **flip to favour high tiers** (1 : 2 : 4) and Tier III becomes available.
