## How to get it
Obsidian is never in the ordinary drop / pack / trade pools.
- **Mainly: mine an Obsidian [[Mining|Ore Block]]** — requires a **Diamond [[Pickaxe]]** or better (Minecraft-faithful).
- A **rare Cave (underground) blind drop** (~1 in 4 Cave wins).
- Beating a **Ruined Portal** the normal way (without lighting it) banks 1 Obsidian.

## Used in
- **Light a Ruined Portal** — spend 1 Obsidian with a [[Flint and Steel]] to open the portal and warp to **the Nether**.
- **Enchanting Table station** — 1 Book + 2 [[Diamond]] + 4 Obsidian (see [[Enchanting]]).
