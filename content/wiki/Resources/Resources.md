Implemented: 13 (9 ores + 3 mob drops + Sticks)

---

Resources are Minecraft materials you stockpile during a run. They live in a **hotbar below your consumables** (counts, not cards) and are the raw input for [[Crafting]]. Counts are per-run — they reset when a run ends.

There are three kinds:
- **Gathered ores** (9) — dropped by beating blinds and found in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]. Always visible in the hotbar (greyed out at 0).
- **Mob drops** (3 — String, Feather, Flint) — creature materials. They do **not** dilute the ore pools: they come from themed blinds, a low-rate loot roll, and the [[Fishing Rod]]. Each feeds a ranged-weapon recipe (the Archery chain).
- **Crafted** (Sticks) — only made at the crafting table; excluded from all drops and packs. Shown only once you own some.

## How you get ores
**Every blind you beat drops resources.** A few bosses have themed drops; everything else uses a tier-roll:

| Source | Drop |
| --- | --- |
| [[Creeper\|The Creeper]] | 2× Coal |
| [[Skeleton\|The Skeleton]] | 1× Iron |
| [[Zombie\|The Zombie]] | 2× Wood |
| [[Spider\|The Spider]] | 2× String (mob drop, on top of its ore) |
| Any other **boss** blind | 2× of a random ore of the highest unlocked tier |
| Any **Small / Big** blind | 1× of a random tier-1 ore |

**Mob drops** are granted *in addition* to the ore above: a themed blind (e.g. Spider → String) always gives its material, and any other blind has a low chance to drop a random mob material you've unlocked. This is how String / Feather / Flint enter a run without diluting the ore pools.

**Tier unlocks by ante:** tier 1 from ante 1, **tier 2 from ante 3**, **tier 3 from ante 6**, **tier 4 (Netherite) from ante 8**. Higher antes can roll the better ores. Your current [[Biomes|biome]] biases *which* ore you roll.

Ores also drop from **[[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]** (tier-weighted 4 : 2 : 1 : 1 for tiers 1 : 2 : 3 : 4), and a [[Pickaxe]] tool can grant bonus ore on your next blind defeat.

> **Mob drops have started (Wave 1 — Archery):** String, Feather and Flint are the first mob materials, each feeding a ranged-weapon recipe ([[Bow]], [[Fishing Rod]], [[Crossbow]], [[Arrow]]). Future waves add more (gunpowder, leather, blaze rods, …) and their craftable ends.
>
> **Planned — mining:** [[Enhancements|Ore Blocks]] spawn on your deck cards (scaling with biome & ante); **play the card to mine it**, and the [[Pickaxe]] boosts what you mine.
