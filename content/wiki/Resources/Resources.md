Implemented: 48 resources (11 gathered ores · 8 mob drops · 3 refined · 16 special · 10 crafted)

---

Resources are the Minecraft materials you stockpile during a run. They live in a **hotbar below your consumables** (counts, not cards) and are the raw input for [[Crafting]], [[Brewing Stand|brewing]], [[Enchanting]], and the Anvil. Counts are per-run — they reset when a run ends.

## The five kinds

- **Gathered ores** (11) — the minerals that flow through the whole loot economy: blind drops, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]], mineable [[Mining|Ore Blocks]], and [[Villager Trading]] bundles. Always visible in the hotbar (greyed out at 0).
- **Mob drops** (8) — creature materials. They do **not** dilute the ore pools: they come from the [[Night]] / [[Cave]] blinds, a low-rate loot roll, and the [[Fishing Rod]]. Each has a `location` (surface / cave / both) gating where it drops.
- **Refined** (3 — [[Iron]], [[Gold]], [[Glass]]) — Furnace outputs only. You smelt [[Raw Iron]] → Iron, [[Raw Gold]] → Gold, and [[Sand]] → Glass. They never drop, pack, mine or trade.
- **Special** (16) — granted only by bespoke code, never in any pool: the Nether/End progression items, the dimension-themed [[Brewing Stand|brewing]] ingredients, and the overworld flowers.
- **Crafted** (10) — made only at the crafting table or a station; excluded from all drops and packs. Shown only once you own some.

## How you get ores

**Every blind you beat drops resources.** [[Wood]] and [[Cobblestone]] are **guaranteed** rolls on every win (biased by environment); metals are a **rare** bonus — you're meant to **mine** them from your deck. A few bosses replace the metal slot with a themed drop:

| Source | Drop |
| --- | --- |
| [[Creeper\|The Creeper]] | 2× [[Coal]] |
| [[Skeleton\|The Skeleton]] | 1× [[Raw Iron]] |
| [[Spider\|The Spider]] | 2× [[String]] (mob drop, on top of its ore) |
| [[Enderman]] | 1× [[Ender Pearl]] |
| [[Blaze]] | 1× [[Blaze Powder]] |
| Any other blind | guaranteed Wood + Cobblestone, plus a rare random-metal bonus |

**Tier unlocks by ante:** tier 1 from ante 1, **tier 2 from ante 3**, **tier 3 from ante 6**, **tier 4 (Netherite) from ante 8**. Higher antes can roll the better ores; your current [[Biomes|biome]] biases *which* ore you roll. The rare metal bonus is more likely (and higher-tier) on Cave and boss wins than at Night.

Ores also drop from **[[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]** (tier-weighted, tier 1 highest), can be bought with [[Emerald]]s at the **[[Villager Trading|Villager]]**, and a [[Pickaxe]] / [[Shovel]] grants a bonus on your next defeat.

**Mob drops** are granted *in addition* to the ore: a themed blind always gives its material, and any other [[Night]] / [[Cave]] win has a low chance to drop a random mob material you've unlocked — String, Feather and Flint (Wave 1 — Archery), then Gunpowder, Bone, Spider Eye, Glow Ink Sac (Wave 2 — Night/Cave) and Leather. This is how creature materials enter a run without diluting the ore pools.

> **Mining is LIVE.** [[Mining|Ore Blocks]] spawn on your deck cards (scaling with [[Biomes|biome]] & ante). Use a [[Pickaxe]] (or [[Axe]]) on a highlighted ore card to mine it — but only if your tool's material meets the block's mining level (Minecraft-faithful: cobblestone/lapis need stone, raw gold/redstone/diamond/emerald need iron, [[Netherite]] needs diamond, [[Obsidian]] needs a **Diamond** pickaxe). [[Wood]] is harvested by the [[Axe]], never the pickaxe.
