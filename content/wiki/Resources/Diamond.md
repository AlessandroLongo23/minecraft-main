## How to get it
- Tier-3 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 6+** (the rarest drop).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] (tier-3, lowest weight — ~1 in 17 per slot).
- Biased high in the [[Snowy Taiga]], [[Basalt Deltas]], [[Central End]], [[End Highlands]] and [[End Barrens]] biomes.

## Used in
- **Diamond tools** — Diamond [[Sword]] (×8 Mult), [[Pickaxe]] (+5 ore), [[Shovel]] (+$10) — the strongest tier of each tool.
