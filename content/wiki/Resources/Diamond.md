## How to get it
- Tier-3 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 6+** (shared with [[Lapis Lazuli]] and [[Redstone]]).
- A rare metal bonus on Cave / boss wins (the high-tier roll).
- Mine a Diamond [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs an Iron [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-3, lowest weight — ~1 in 17 per slot).
- Biased high in the [[Snowy Taiga]], [[Basalt Deltas]] and [[The End]] biomes.

## Used in
- **Diamond tools** — Diamond [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] (and the Diamond [[Axe]]) — the strongest *craftable* tier (Netherite is an Anvil upgrade).
- **Enchanting Table station** — 1 Book + 2 Diamond + 4 [[Obsidian]] (see [[Enchanting]]).
- **Diamond [[Sheets|Sheet]]** — forged at the Anvil.
