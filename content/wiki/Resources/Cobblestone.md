## How to get it
- **A guaranteed roll on every blind win** (averaging ~1–2), biased higher in the [[Cave]] (underground) than at [[Night]] (surface).
- Mine a Cobblestone [[Mining|Ore Block]] (any [[Pickaxe]]).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-1).
- Biased high in the [[Plains]] and [[Savanna]] biomes.

## Used in
- **Stone tools** — Stone [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] (and the Stone [[Axe]]).
- **Furnace station** — 8 Cobblestone (smelts raw ore → ingots).
- **Brewing Stand station** — 3 Cobblestone + 1 [[Blaze Rod]].
- **[[TNT]]** — 4 [[Gunpowder]] + 1 Cobblestone.
