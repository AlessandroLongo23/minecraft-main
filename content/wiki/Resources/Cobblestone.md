## How to get it
- Tier-1 roll from any Small/Big blind, or a low-ante boss.
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] (tier-1, highest weight).
- Biased high in the [[Plains]] and [[Savanna]] biomes.

## Used in
- **Stone tools** — Stone [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] (the "Stone" tier of each tool is made from Cobblestone).
