## How to get it
- Tier-3 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 6+** (shared with [[Diamond]] and [[Redstone]]).
- A rare metal bonus on Cave / boss wins (the high-tier roll).
- Mine a Lapis [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs a Stone [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-3, lowest weight — ~1 in 17 per slot).

## Used in
- **[[Enchanting]]** — every enchant spends Lapis *alongside* XP levels, Minecraft-style. Applying an
  [[Enchanting Books|Enchanting Book]] to a tool costs **1 / 2 / 3** Lapis for a tier I / II / III
  book; enchanting a playing card costs the same (the flat **Unbreaking** edition costs **1**).
  Without enough Lapis the **Enchant** button stays greyed out.
- **Lapis [[Sheets|Sheet]]** — forged at the Anvil.
