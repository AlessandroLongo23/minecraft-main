## How to get it
**Crafting only.** Made at the [[Crafting]] table from [[Spider Eye]] + [[Sugar]] + [[Brown Mushroom]]. Never drops and never appears in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]; shown in the hotbar only once you own some.

## Used in
- **[[Brewing Stand|Brewing]]** — turns an Awkward Potion into **Splash Harming**, and corrupts a Night Vision potion into **Invisibility** (see [[Potions]]).
