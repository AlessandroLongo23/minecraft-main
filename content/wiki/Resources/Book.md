## How to get it
**Crafting only.** Made at the [[Crafting]] table from 3 [[Paper]] + 1 [[Leather]]. Never drops and never appears in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]; shown in the hotbar only once you own some.

## Used in
- **Enchanting Table station** — 1 Book + 2 [[Diamond]] + 4 [[Obsidian]] builds the table.
- At the **Enchanting Table** a Book is consumed to generate an **Enchanting Book** (with [[Lapis Lazuli]] + XP levels). See [[Enchanting]].
