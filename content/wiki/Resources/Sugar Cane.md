## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win in the Overworld** can drop Sugar Cane (shared with [[Resources/Carrot|Carrot]], [[Melon Slice]], [[Brown Mushroom]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Sugar** — 1 Sugar Cane → 1 Sugar (a [[Brewing Stand|brewing]] intermediate).
- **[[Paper]]** — 3 Sugar Cane → 3 Paper (the [[Enchanting]] book chain).
