## How to get it
- Low chance from any blind win (mob loot roll) — there is no dedicated chicken blind.
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **[[Arrow]]** — 1 [[Flint]] + 1 [[Sticks|Stick]] + 1 Feather.
