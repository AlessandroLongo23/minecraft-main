## How to get it
Blaze Powder is never in the ordinary drop / pack / trade pools.
- **1× from beating the [[Blaze]] blind** (the bootstrap source).
- A chance on any win **while you are in the Nether**.
- **Craft from a [[Blaze Rod]]** — 1 Blaze Rod → 2 Blaze Powder.

## Used in
- **[[Eye of Ender]]** — 1 [[Ender Pearl]] + 1 Blaze Powder (the path to the End).
- **[[Brewing Stand|Brewing]]** — fuels the Brewing Stand, and brews the **Strength** potion.
