## How to get it
**Crafting only.** Sticks never drop from blinds and never appear in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] — you make them at the [[Crafting]] table:

```
. W .
. W .
. . .
```
**2 [[Wood]] (stacked) → 2 Sticks.**

The hotbar only shows Sticks once you own at least one.

## Used in
- **Every tool recipe** — the handle of each [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] and the [[Torch]].
