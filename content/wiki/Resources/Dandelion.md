## How to get it
- A small **Overworld** trickle on blind wins (the organic roll), shared with [[Poppy]], [[Cornflower]] and [[Oxeye Daisy]].
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Composter fuel → [[Bone Meal]]** — flowers are pure organics with no other use; feed them to the Composter to make Bone Meal.
