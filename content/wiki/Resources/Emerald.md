## How to get it
- Tier-2 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 3+**, and a rare metal bonus on Night / Cave / boss wins.
- Mine an Emerald [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs an Iron [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] (tier-2 weight).

## Used in
- **[[Villager Trading]]** — Emerald is the trading **currency**, not a crafting material. Spend emeralds in the shop's Villager section to buy resource bundles and tools you can't get directly.
- **Emerald [[Sheets|Sheet]]** — forged at the Anvil.
