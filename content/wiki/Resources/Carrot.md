## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win in the Overworld** can drop Carrot (shared with [[Sugar Cane]], [[Melon Slice]], [[Brown Mushroom]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Golden Carrot** — Carrot + [[Gold]] → Golden Carrot, which brews the **Night Vision** potion. See [[Brewing Stand]] / [[Potions]].
