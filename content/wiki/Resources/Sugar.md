## How to get it
**Crafting only.** Made at the [[Crafting]] table from 1 [[Sugar Cane]] → 1 Sugar. Never drops and never appears in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]; shown in the hotbar only once you own some.

## Used in
- **Swiftness potion** — added to an Awkward Potion at the [[Brewing Stand]].
- **Fermented Spider Eye** — [[Spider Eye]] + Sugar + [[Brown Mushroom]] (see [[Potions]]).
