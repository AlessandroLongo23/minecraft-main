## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win while in the Nether** can drop Blaze Rod (shared with [[Nether Wart]], [[Ghast Tear]], [[Glowstone Dust]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **[[Blaze Powder]]** — 1 Blaze Rod → 2 Blaze Powder.
- **Brewing Stand station** — 1 Blaze Rod + 3 [[Cobblestone]] builds the [[Brewing Stand]].
