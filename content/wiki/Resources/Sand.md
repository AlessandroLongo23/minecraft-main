## How to get it
- Tier-1 roll from any blind win (part of the basic metal pool, alongside [[Coal]]).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-1).
- Sand is **not** a mineable Ore Block — it isn't a block you mine with a [[Pickaxe]].

## Used in
- **Smelt into [[Glass]]** at the [[Crafting|Furnace]] — sand is the start of the glass chain.
