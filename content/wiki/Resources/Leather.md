## How to get it
- **Surface-only.** Low chance from a [[Night]] (surface) blind win only — it never drops underground.
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **Book** — 3 [[Paper]] + 1 Leather → a Book, the first step of the [[Enchanting]] chain.
