## How to get it
- Low chance from any blind win (mob loot roll) — Flint comes from gravel, so it has no dedicated blind.
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **[[Arrow]]** — 1 Flint + 1 [[Sticks|Stick]] + 1 [[Feather]].
- **[[Flint and Steel]]** — 1 [[Iron]] + 1 Flint (lights a Ruined Portal → the Nether).
