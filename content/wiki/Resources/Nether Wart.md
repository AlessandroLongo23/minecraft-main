## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win while in the Nether** can drop Nether Wart (shared with [[Blaze Rod]], [[Ghast Tear]], [[Glowstone Dust]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Awkward Potion** — Nether Wart is the **brewing base**: the first ingredient fed into a Water Bottle at the [[Brewing Stand]], from which every other potion is brewed. See [[Potions]].
