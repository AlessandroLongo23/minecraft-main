## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win while in the Nether** can drop Ghast Tear (shared with [[Nether Wart]], [[Blaze Rod]], [[Glowstone Dust]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Healing potion** — added to an Awkward Potion at the [[Brewing Stand]] to brew **Healing** (see [[Potions]]).
