## How to get it
**Smelting only.** Glass never drops from blinds, packs, mining or trading — you make it at the [[Crafting|Furnace]] by smelting [[Sand]].

## Used in
- **Glass Bottle** and **Water Bottle** — the base containers for [[Brewing Stand|brewing]] [[Potions]].
- **Glass [[Sheets|Sheet]]** — the one Sheet crafted at the [[Crafting]] table (3 Glass in a row).
