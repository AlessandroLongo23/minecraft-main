## How to get it
**Crafting only.** Made at the [[Crafting]] table from 3 [[Glass]] (→ 3 Water Bottles). Never drops and never appears in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]]; shown in the hotbar only once you own some.

## Used in
- **The base of every potion** — a Water Bottle is the starting container loaded into the [[Brewing Stand]]; add [[Nether Wart]] for an Awkward Potion, then brew on from there. See [[Potions]].
