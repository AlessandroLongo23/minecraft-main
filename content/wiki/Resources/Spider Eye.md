## How to get it
- **Cave-exclusive.** Low chance from a [[Cave]] (underground) blind win only — it never drops on the surface.
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **[[Brewing Stand|Brewing]]** — turns an Awkward Potion into Splash Poison; combine with Sugar + Brown Mushroom to make **Fermented Spider Eye** (→ Harming / Invisibility).
- Helps **gate tool [[Enchanting|enchants]]**.
