## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win while in the Nether** can drop Glowstone Dust (shared with [[Nether Wart]], [[Blaze Rod]], [[Ghast Tear]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **[[Brewing Stand|Brewing]]** — the **Potency** modifier; adds it to a finished potion to make a stronger (Level II) version.
