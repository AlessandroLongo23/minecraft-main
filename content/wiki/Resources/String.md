## How to get it
- **2× from beating [[Spider|The Spider]]** (themed mob drop).
- Low chance from any other blind win (mob loot roll).
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].
