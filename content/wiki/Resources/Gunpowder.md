## How to get it
- Low chance from any blind win (mob loot roll) — droppable in **both** the [[Night]] and [[Cave]] environments.
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **[[TNT]]** — 4 Gunpowder + 1 [[Cobblestone]].
- **[[Firework]]** — 1 Gunpowder + 1 [[Sticks|Stick]].
- **Splash [[Potions]]** — the finisher for Splash Poison / Splash Harming at the [[Brewing Stand]].
- Helps **gate tier-2 / tier-3 tool [[Enchanting|enchants]]**.
