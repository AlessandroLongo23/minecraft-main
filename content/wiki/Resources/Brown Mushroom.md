## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win in the Overworld** can drop Brown Mushroom (shared with [[Sugar Cane]], [[Resources/Carrot|Carrot]], [[Melon Slice]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Fermented Spider Eye** — [[Spider Eye]] + Sugar + Brown Mushroom. Used to brew Harming / Invisibility potions at the [[Brewing Stand]] (see [[Potions]]).
