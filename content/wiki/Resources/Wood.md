## How to get it
- **2× from beating [[Zombie|The Zombie]]** (themed drop).
- Tier-1 roll from any Small/Big blind, or a low-ante boss.
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] (tier-1, highest weight).

## Used in
- **[[Sticks]]** — 2 Wood → 2 Sticks (the base of every tool recipe).
- **Wooden tools** — Wooden [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]].
