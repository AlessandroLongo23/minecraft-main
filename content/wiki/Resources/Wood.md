## How to get it
- **A guaranteed roll on every blind win** (averaging ~1–2), biased higher at [[Night]] (surface) than in the [[Cave]] (underground — no trees underground).
- Harvested by the **[[Axe]]** (Wood is gathered with an axe, never the [[Pickaxe]]).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-1).

## Used in
- **[[Sticks]]** — 2 Wood → 2 Sticks (the base of every tool recipe).
- **Wooden tools** — Wooden [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] (and the Wooden [[Axe]]).
- **Chest station** — 8 Wood (expands your inventory capacity).
- **Composter station** — 7 Wood (turns flowers into [[Bone Meal]]).
