## How to get it
- **Cave-exclusive.** Low chance from a [[Cave]] (underground) blind win only (the Glow Squid) — it never drops on the surface.
- Reeled in by the **[[Fishing Rod]]**.
- Mob drops never appear in the ore tier roll or [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **[[Torch]]** — a Glow Ink Sac above a [[Sticks|Stick]] crafts a Torch and yields **2** (coal yields 1). The cave's own light source.
