## How to get it
**Brewing only.** Made at the [[Brewing Stand]] by adding [[Nether Wart]] to a [[Water Bottle]]. Never drops and never appears in [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]].

## Used in
- **The base of every potion** — almost all [[Potions]] are brewed by adding one more ingredient to an Awkward Potion (Sugar → Swiftness, [[Blaze Powder]] → Strength, Glistering Melon → Instant Health, and so on). See [[Brewing Stand]].
