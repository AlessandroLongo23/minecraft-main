## How to get it
- Tier-3 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 6+** (shared with [[Diamond]] and [[Lapis Lazuli]]).
- A rare metal bonus on Cave / boss wins (the high-tier roll).
- Mine a Redstone [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs an Iron [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-3).

## Used in
- **[[Brewing Stand|Brewing]]** — the **Duration** modifier; adds it to a finished potion to make a longer-lasting drinkable.
- **Redstone [[Sheets|Sheet]]** — forged at the Anvil.
