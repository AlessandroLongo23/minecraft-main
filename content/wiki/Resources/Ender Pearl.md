## How to get it
Ender Pearls are never in the ordinary drop / pack / trade pools.
- **1× from beating the [[Enderman]] blind** (the bootstrap source).
- A chance on any win **while you are in the End**.
- A chance on any win **while in the Nether** (warped-forest Endermen / Piglin bartering), so the End stays reachable.

## Used in
- **[[Eye of Ender]]** — 1 Ender Pearl + 1 [[Blaze Powder]]. Crafting and following the Eye-of-Ender trail leads to **the End**.
