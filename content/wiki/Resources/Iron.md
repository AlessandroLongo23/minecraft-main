## How to get it
**Smelting only.** Iron ingots no longer drop, pack, mine or trade directly — you obtain **[[Raw Iron]]** and smelt it at the [[Crafting|Furnace]].

## Used in
- **Iron tools** — Iron [[Sword]], [[Pickaxe]], [[Shovel]] (and the Iron [[Axe]]).
- **[[Crossbow]]** — 3 [[String]] + 2 [[Sticks]] + 1 Iron.
- **[[Flint and Steel]]** — 1 Iron + 1 [[Flint]] (lights a Ruined Portal).
- **Anvil station** — building the Anvil costs Iron (forges Sheets, combines & upgrades tools).
- **Iron [[Sheets|Sheet]]** — forged at the Anvil.
