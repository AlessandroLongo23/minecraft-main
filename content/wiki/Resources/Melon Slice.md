## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win in the Overworld** can drop Melon Slice (shared with [[Sugar Cane]], [[Resources/Carrot|Carrot]], [[Brown Mushroom]]).
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **Glistering Melon** — Melon Slice + [[Gold]] → Glistering Melon, which brews the **Instant Health** potion. See [[Brewing Stand]] / [[Potions]].
