## How to get it
- Tier-2 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 3+**, and a rare metal bonus on Night / Cave / boss wins.
- Mine a Raw Gold [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs an Iron [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-2).

## Used in
- **Smelt into [[Gold]]** at the [[Crafting|Furnace]] — raw gold is the only way to obtain Gold ingots.
