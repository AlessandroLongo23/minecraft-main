## How to get it
- Tier-4 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 8+** (the rarest drop, above Diamond).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] (tier-4, lowest weight — on par with Diamond per slot).
- Biased high in the [[Nether Wastes]], [[Basalt Deltas]], [[Crimson Forest]] and [[Soul Sand Valley]] Nether biomes.

## Used in
- **Netherite tools** — Netherite [[Sword]] (×16 Mult), [[Pickaxe]] (+6 ore), [[Shovel]] (+$15) — the strongest tier of each tool, one step above Diamond.
