## How to get it
- Tier-4 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 8+** (the rarest, above Diamond).
- A rare metal bonus on Cave / boss wins (the high-tier roll).
- Mine a Netherite [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs a Diamond [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-4, lowest weight).
- Biased high in the [[Nether Wastes]], [[Basalt Deltas]], [[Crimson Forest]] and [[Soul Sand Valley]] Nether biomes.

## Used in
- **Netherite tools** — the strongest tier of each tool, one step above Diamond. You can't craft these at the table: craft the **Diamond** tool, then **upgrade it at the Anvil** (Diamond tool + 1 Netherite → the Netherite tool, keeping its enchants and durability).
