## How to get it
- **1× from beating [[Skeleton|The Skeleton]]** (themed drop).
- Tier-2 roll from bosses **once you reach ante 3+**, and a rare metal bonus on Night / Cave / boss wins.
- Mine a Raw Iron [[Mining|Ore Block]] (needs a Stone [[Pickaxe]] or better).
- From [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] and [[Villager Trading]] bundles (tier-2).

## Used in
- **Smelt into [[Iron]]** at the [[Crafting|Furnace]] — raw iron is the only way to obtain Iron ingots.
