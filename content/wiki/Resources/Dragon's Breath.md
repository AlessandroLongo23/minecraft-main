## How to get it
Brewing ingredients only drop from blind wins **in the dimension they belong to**, one ingredient per win.
- **A blind win while in the End** can drop Dragon's Breath — it is the End's sole ingredient drop.
- Never in the ore / mob pools, [[Booster Packs|Loot Chests]] or [[Villager Trading]].

## Used in
- **[[Brewing Stand|Brewing]]** — the **Lingering** modifier; adds it to a Splash potion to make a lingering throwable. See [[Potions]].
