Potions are a Minecraft-style consumable category, **brewed** at the [[Brewing Stand]] rather than bought. Each is either **Drunk** (a buff on you or the run — some restore [[Health|hearts]]) or **Thrown** (a Splash debuff on the blind) — the card's button says **Drink** or **Throw** instead of "Use". Potions live in the Minecraft consumable area alongside [[Tools]] and [[Food]], and can also turn up in a **[[Booster Packs|Potion Pack]]**.

Brew modifiers tune a potion as you make it: **Glowstone Dust** → stronger (Level II), **Redstone** → longer (drinkables, more blinds), **Dragon's Breath** → lingering (throwables carry into the next blind).

## Brewing
See [[Brewing Stand]] for the full chain. In short: smelt Sand → Glass and craft a **Glass Bottle**, fill it into a **Water Bottle**, add **Nether Wart** for an **Awkward Potion** (the base for everything), then add the effect ingredient. Brewing burns **Blaze Powder** as fuel. Throwables (Poison, Harming) need **Gunpowder** to become Splash potions, and the corruption potions (Invisibility, Harming) use **Fermented Spider Eye**.
