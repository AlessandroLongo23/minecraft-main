## Effect
Drink during a blind to **take no boss-blind damage** for the rest of this blind — the boss can't hurt your [[Health|hearts]] while you're invisible.

- **+Long ([[Enchanting Books|Redstone]]):** protection lasts **2 blinds** instead of 1.

It accepts the **Redstone** (Long) modifier only — no Level II or lingering.

## How to brew it
A **corruption** brew: take a finished **Night Vision** potion and add **Fermented Spider Eye** at the [[Brewing Stand]] (Fermented Spider Eye is crafted from Spider Eye + Sugar + Brown Mushroom). See [[Brewing Stand]] for the chain and fuel cost.
