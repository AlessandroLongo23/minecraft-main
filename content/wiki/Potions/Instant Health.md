| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Use | **Drink** |

| Brewed from | Awkward Potion **+ Glistering Melon** |
| Booster tier | 2 |
| Center id | `c_balacraft_potion_instant_health` |

## Effect
Drink to immediately restore **3 [[Health|hearts]]** of health (up to your maximum).

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** restores **4 hearts** instead of 3.

It accepts the **Glowstone Dust** (Level II) modifier only.

## How to brew it
At the [[Brewing Stand]]: start from an **Awkward Potion** and add **Glistering Melon**. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the full chain and fuel cost.
