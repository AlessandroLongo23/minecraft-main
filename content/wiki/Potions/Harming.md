## Effect
Throw at the blind to **instantly cut its score requirement by −50%** — a one-shot burst, the opposite of [[Poison]]'s slow drain.

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** the cut is **−65%** instead of −50%.
- **+Linger ([[Enchanting Books|Dragon's Breath]]):** a smaller **−25%** cut also hits the **next** blind.

## How to brew it
A two-step **Splash** brew at the [[Brewing Stand]]: add **Fermented Spider Eye** to an Awkward Potion (making a harming base), then finish it with **Gunpowder** to make it throwable. Fermented Spider Eye is crafted from Spider Eye + Sugar + Brown Mushroom. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the chain and fuel cost.
