## Effect
Drink for a **regeneration buff**: restore **1 [[Health|heart]]** now and at the start of each of the next **3** blinds.

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** heals **2 hearts** per tick instead of 1.
- **+Long ([[Enchanting Books|Redstone]]):** the regen lasts **4** blinds instead of 3.

The two modifiers stack: a Level II, Long Healing potion heals 2 hearts at the start of 4 blinds.

## How to brew it
At the [[Brewing Stand]]: start from an **Awkward Potion** and add **Ghast Tear**. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the full chain and fuel cost.
