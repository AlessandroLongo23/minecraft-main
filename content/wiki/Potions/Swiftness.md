## Effect
Drink to instantly **change your [[Biomes|biome]]** — a biome-select screen opens offering **3** of the biomes available in your current dimension.

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** offers **4** biomes instead of 3.

It accepts the **Glowstone Dust** (Level II) modifier only — no Redstone or Dragon's Breath.

## How to brew it
At the [[Brewing Stand]]: start from an **Awkward Potion** and add **Sugar**. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the full Glass Bottle → Water Bottle → Awkward Potion chain, and the fuel cost (Blaze Powder).
