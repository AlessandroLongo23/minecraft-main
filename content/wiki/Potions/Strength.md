## Effect
Drink during a blind to **balance the scoring** — this blind's Chips and Mult are pulled **10%** toward their average each scored hand, softening a lopsided blind (great against a blind that crushes either chips or mult).

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** pulls **20%** toward the average instead of 10%.
- **+Long ([[Enchanting Books|Redstone]]):** the effect lasts **2 blinds** instead of 1.

## How to brew it
At the [[Brewing Stand]]: start from an **Awkward Potion** and add **Blaze Powder**. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the full chain and fuel cost.
