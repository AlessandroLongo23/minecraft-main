*Splash potion — thrown at the blind, brewed at the [[Brewing Stand]]*

![[potion_poison.png|105]]

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Use | **Throw** |
| When usable | In a blind (while selecting a hand) |
| Brewed from | Awkward Potion **+ Spider Eye + Gunpowder** |
| Booster tier | 2 |
| Center id | `c_balacraft_potion_poison` |

## Effect
Throw at the blind to **poison it**: every hand you play or discard cuts the blind's score requirement by **−10%** (the throw itself counts as the first tick). The poison ticks for the rest of the blind.

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** each tick cuts **−15%** instead of −10%.
- **+Linger ([[Enchanting Books|Dragon's Breath]]):** the poison **carries into the next blind** too (2 blinds of ticks).

## How to brew it
A two-step **Splash** brew at the [[Brewing Stand]]: add **Spider Eye** to an Awkward Potion (making a poison base), then finish it with **Gunpowder** to make it throwable. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the chain and fuel cost.
