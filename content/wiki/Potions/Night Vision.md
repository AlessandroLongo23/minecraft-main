## Effect
Drink during a blind to **reveal the next 3 cards** you'll draw — they're shown face-up beside your deck so you can plan ahead.

- **+II ([[Enchanting Books|Glowstone Dust]]):** reveals the next **5** cards instead of 3.
- **+Long ([[Enchanting Books|Redstone]]):** the reveal lasts **2 blinds** instead of 1.

## How to brew it
At the [[Brewing Stand]]: start from an **Awkward Potion** and add **Golden Carrot**. See [[Brewing Stand]] for the full chain and fuel cost.
