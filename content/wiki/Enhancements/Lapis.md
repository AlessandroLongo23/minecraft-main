## Effect
A **Lapis** card grants **[[Experience|XP]] every time it is played** — about **a quarter of a level**
each play. The amount scales with your current level (it's a fraction of what the next level costs), so
it stays meaningful deep into a run instead of fizzling to a rounding error. It fires even when the card
isn't part of the scoring hand, so it's your steady, repeatable XP engine — the more you play it, the
faster you level.

Lapis lazuli is Minecraft's enchanting material, so a Lapis card naturally feeds the [[Enchanting]] loop.

## Notes
- One enhancement per card; applied by the [[Sheets|Lapis Sheet]] (which you forge at the Anvil from
  [[Lapis Lazuli]]).
- The [[Soul Seal]] also grants XP on play (a flat +5) — the rarer, seal-applied version. A card can hold
  both a Lapis enhancement *and* a Soul Seal for two XP sources at once.
- Feeds the [[Experience]] bar you spend at the [[Enchanting Table]] (generating [[Enchanting Books]]).
