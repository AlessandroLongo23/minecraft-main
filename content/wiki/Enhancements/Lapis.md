## Effect
A **Lapis** card grants **+3 [[Experience|XP]]** every time it is **played**. Lapis lazuli is the
enchanting material in Minecraft, so a Lapis card is your steady, repeatable XP engine — the more you
play it, the more you level.

## Notes
- One enhancement per card; applied by a Tarot (planned).
- The [[Soul Seal]] also grants XP on play (+5) — that's the rarer, seal-applied version. Stack a Lapis
  card with a Soul Seal for both.
- Feeds the [[Experience]] bar you spend on [[Enchanting]].
