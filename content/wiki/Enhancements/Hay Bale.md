## Effect
A **Hay Bale** card **restores +1 [[Hunger|hunger]] pip** each time it **scores**. In Minecraft hay
bales feed animals; here they feed you a little every time you play the card.

## Notes
- One enhancement per card; applied by a Tarot (planned).
- Pairs with the survival loop: steady hunger from cards you play, on top of [[Food]] you eat. Keeping
  hunger high passively heals [[Health|hearts]].
