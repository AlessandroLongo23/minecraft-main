## Effect
Obsidian is **not** a held-in-hand multiplier card and is **not** applied by a Tarot. It is an
[[Ore Blocks|Ore Block]]: it spawns on cards in your deck (tier-3 depth, so from around ante 6), and
you **mine it by Using a [[Pickaxe]] on the highlighted card**. Like real Minecraft, obsidian can only
be broken with a **Diamond pickaxe (or Netherite)** — a lesser pickaxe flashes *"Need a better
pickaxe."* Mining it yields **1 [[Obsidian (resource)|Obsidian]]** resource (the [[Nether]]-portal fuel
spent by [[Flint and Steel]]).

## Notes
- One enhancement per card; obsidian blocks never spawn on a card that already has an enhancement.
- Obsidian is otherwise **never** in the drop / pack / villager / seal / tag pools — mining its block
  is the main way to get it (the bespoke Cave-blind and Ruined-Portal grants still apply).
- Gating it to the Diamond pickaxe mirrors Minecraft's progression: get diamonds → craft a Diamond
  pickaxe → mine obsidian → light a [[Nether]] portal.
