## Effect
An **Obsidian** card is the toughest block in the game:
- **Indestructible** — immune to boss [[New Bosses|blind]] effects that would destroy or debuff it.
- **×1.5 Mult while held in hand** (reuses the vanilla **Steel** card's ratio).

A defensive anchor: keep it in hand for the multiplier, and don't fear the [[Creeper]],
[[Spider]], or other destructive bosses.

## Notes
- One enhancement per card; applied by a Tarot (planned).
- Overlaps thematically with the [[Unbreaking]] edition (which *protects* a card) — Obsidian *is* the
  protection, baked into the card body, plus the held-Mult.
