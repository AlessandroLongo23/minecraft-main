## Effect
Obsidian is **not** applied by a Tarot or [[Sheets|Sheet]] and is not a held-in-hand multiplier. It is an
[[Ore Blocks|Ore Block]]: it spawns on cards in your deck (tier-3 depth, so from around ante 6) and you
**[[Mining|mine]] it by Using a [[Pickaxe]] on the highlighted card**. Like real Minecraft, obsidian can
only be broken with a **Diamond pickaxe (or Netherite)** — a lesser pickaxe flashes *"Need a better
pickaxe."* Mining it yields **1 [[Resources/Obsidian|Obsidian]]** resource.

## Notes
- One enhancement per card; obsidian blocks never spawn on a card that already has an enhancement.
- Obsidian is otherwise **never** in the drop / pack / villager / seal / tag pools — mining its block is
  the main way to get it (bespoke Cave-blind and Ruined-Portal grants still apply).
- Gating it to the Diamond pickaxe mirrors Minecraft's progression and the mod's [[Dimensions]] access:
  get diamonds → craft a Diamond pickaxe → mine obsidian → spend it with [[Flint and Steel]] to reach the
  Nether.
