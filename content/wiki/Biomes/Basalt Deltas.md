The richest **[[Netherite]]** source in the game (the heaviest weighting on the tier-4 ore), also biased toward [[Cobblestone]], [[Diamond]] and [[Redstone]]. It is the only place the **[[Magma Lord]]** boss can appear (added to the pool while you're here).

## How you reach the Nether
- Light a **Ruined Portal** blind with **[[Flint and Steel]]** while holding **1 [[Obsidian]]** — see [[Dimensions]].
- Or start a run with the [[Nether Deck]].
