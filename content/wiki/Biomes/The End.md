The End is now a **single biome** (matching Minecraft's central island — the outer-island biomes were retired). Biased toward [[Diamond]] and [[Gold]], it is the only place the **[[Ender Sentinel]]** boss appears (added to the pool while you're here).

Because the End holds only one biome, you don't get further biome choices once you arrive — you simply stay in The End.

## How you reach the End
- Craft an **[[Eye of Ender]]** and use it to read the **Eye-of-Ender trail**. Each Eye marks one option at the next biome selection. Follow the marked path for **three consecutive antes** — (1) a random biome, (2) the **Stronghold**, (3) The End — to arrive. See [[Dimensions]].
- Or start a run with the [[End Deck]] (begins you directly in the End).
- Skipping an ante, or picking an unmarked option, resets the trail to step 1.
