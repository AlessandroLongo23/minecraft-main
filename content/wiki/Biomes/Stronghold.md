Ancient stone halls where the Eye of Ender points. The Stronghold is a **ritual-only** biome: it never appears in a normal biome roll. It shows up **only** as the marked second step of the **Eye-of-Ender trail**, the waypoint toward The End.

## How you reach it
- Use an **[[Eye of Ender]]** to arm the trail, then pick the marked option each ante. The marked second step is always the Stronghold; the marked third step is **[[The End]]**. See [[Dimensions]].
- Skipping an ante, or picking an unmarked option, resets the trail.
