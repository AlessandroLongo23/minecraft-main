A welcoming +$3 on arrival, with a [[Gold]]/[[Coal]] bias and — like every Nether biome — a chance at the tier-4 **[[Netherite]]** ore.

## How you reach the Nether
- Light a **Ruined Portal** blind (a structure that can appear on any Overworld Night/Cave blind) with **[[Flint and Steel]]** while holding **1 [[Obsidian]]**. This spends the Obsidian, instantly beats the blind, and warps you to a random Nether biome. See [[Dimensions]].
- Or start a run with the [[Nether Deck]].
