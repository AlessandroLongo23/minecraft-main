## Effect
Select **1 card** in your hand, then use the Firework Rocket to permanently give it **+100 Chips**, plus an extra **+50 Chips for every [[Gunpowder]] you currently own**. The more Gunpowder you hoard, the harder each firework hits — it rewards a gunpowder-stockpiling build. The card's live tooltip shows the total it would grant right now. Consumed on use.

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table — 1 [[Gunpowder]] over 1 [[Sticks|Stick]] (the body):

```
. g .
. S .
. . .
```
(g = Gunpowder, S = Stick.) A [[Gunpowder]] sink — see [[Crafting]].
