## Effect
**Peek the shop.** Using a Torch in the shop opens a one-time preview showing the **next two rerolls** and **the contents of every booster pack** currently on offer. After the peek the Torch is consumed, so you can't reopen it.

## Notes
- Can only be used **in the shop** (it does nothing during a blind).
- A great pairing with the [[Booster Packs]] — scout a pack before you buy it.

## Recipe
Minecraft torch shape — **[[Coal]] directly above a [[Sticks|Stick]]**:

```
. C .
. S .
. . .
```
(C = Coal, S = Stick.) See [[Crafting]].
