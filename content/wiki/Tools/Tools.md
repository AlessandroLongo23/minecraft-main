Implemented: 4 craftable tool types (24 cards) + 7 consumable tools

---

Tools are **craft-only cards**. They never roll in the shop — you make them at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table from ores, [[Sticks]] and mob drops. They *do* appear in the collection. There are two kinds: the four **craftable tool types** (each with 6 material tiers and a durability bar) and a set of **one-shot consumable tools**.

## Craftable tools
Each comes in **6 material tiers** (Wooden → Stone → Iron → Golden → Diamond → Netherite). The material sets how strong the effect is, the **Uses** (durability) budget, and the cost.

| Tool | What it does | Tiers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Sword]] | ×Mult on every hand for the rest of the Blind (Diamond/Netherite raise Mult to a power) | 6 materials |
| [[Pickaxe]] | Mine highlighted [[Mining\|Ore Block]] cards your tier can break | 6 materials |
| [[Shovel]] | ~50% chance to find +$N on the next blind defeated | 6 materials |
| [[Axe]] | Dual: chop extra wood from Wood Ore Blocks, or used alone is a +Chips/×Mult weapon | 6 materials |

## Consumable tools
One-shot cards (no durability) — used once and consumed.

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [[Torch]] | Peek the shop's next rerolls and pack contents |
| [[Arrow]] | Permanently give 1 card +Mult |
| [[Bone Meal]] | Grow up to 3 cards (+Chips) and buff their poker hand |
| [[TNT]] | Destroy a card and the cards on either side of it |
| [[Firework]] | Load a card with Chips, scaling with Gunpowder held |
| [[Flint and Steel]] | Light a Ruined Portal blind → warp to the Nether |
| [[Eye of Ender]] | Follow the trail across 3 antes → reach The End |

## How tools work
- **Craft-only** — they can't be bought; craft them at the [[Crafting]] table (or unlock the recipe via stations like the [[Composter]] / [[Smelting|Furnace]]).
- **Craftable tools are persistent & multi-use** — a tool stays in your consumable area and has a **Uses** budget set by its material (Wooden 1 → Netherite 5). Each use spends one Use; at **0 Uses it breaks**. Usable **once per Blind**, and only **during a blind**.
- **One per type per Blind** — at most one Sword, one Pickaxe, one Shovel and one Axe each Blind. You can mix across types, but not two of the same type.
- **Mining is live** — the Pickaxe and Axe mine [[Mining\|Ore Block]] cards highlighted in your hand, gated by the tool's Minecraft mining level. The Axe also doubles as a weapon when used alone. See [[Mining]].
- **Enchantable** — apply [[Enchanting Books]] to boost a tool: **Sharpness** (Sword), **Fortune** (Pickaxe / Shovel), and **Durability** (all). See [[Enchanting]].
- **Anvil upgrade & combine** — Netherite tools can't be crafted at the table; craft the **Diamond** tool and **upgrade** it at the [[Anvil]] → Upgrade tab (Diamond tool + 1 [[Netherite]]). The Anvil's **Combine** tab merges two same type+material tools to stack their enchants and pool their Uses.
- Tools now have real per-tool, per-material art with a **live durability bar**; enchanted tools also get a coloured glint.
