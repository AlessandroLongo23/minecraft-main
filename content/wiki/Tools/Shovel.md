*Craft-only Tool consumable — 6 material variants*

## Effect
When used, the Shovel **arms a one-shot**: the next time you **defeat a blind**, you earn **+$N**. N scales with the material. It's a **persistent, multi-use** tool: usable **once per Blind**, it spends one **Use** each time and **breaks** when it runs out.

**Enchantable:** [[Fortune]] adds a random bonus to each payout; [[Durability]] raises its Uses. See [[Enchanting]].

## Recipe
Minecraft shovel shape — **1 of the material on top, 2 [[Sticks|Sticks]] as the handle**:

```
. M .
. S .
. S .
```
(M = material ore, S = Stick.) See [[Crafting]].
