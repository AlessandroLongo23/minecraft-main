*Craft-only Tool — 6 material variants*

![[tool_shovel_diamond.png|105]]

## Effect
When used, the Shovel **digs** — a gamble with a **~50% chance to find +$N** the next time you defeat a blind (nothing on a miss). N scales with the material. It's a **persistent, multi-use** tool: usable **once per Blind**, it spends one **Use** each time (hit or miss) and **breaks** when it runs out. At most **one Shovel** per Blind.

**Enchantable:** [[Enchanting Books/Fortune\|Fortune]] raises the **find odds** (+15% per tier), not the payout; [[Enchanting Books/Durability\|Durability]] raises its Uses. See [[Enchanting]].

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table. Minecraft shovel shape — **1 of the material on top, 2 [[Sticks|Sticks]] as the handle**:

```
. M .
. S .
. S .
```
(M = material ore, S = Stick.)

> **Netherite is the exception.** Like in Minecraft, the Netherite Shovel can't be crafted at the table. Craft the **Diamond** Shovel, then **upgrade** it at the [[Anvil]] → Upgrade tab (Diamond tool + 1 [[Netherite]] → Netherite tool). The upgrade keeps the tool's enchants and durability.
