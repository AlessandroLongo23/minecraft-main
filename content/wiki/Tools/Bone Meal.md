## Effect
Select **up to 3 cards** in your hand, then use the Bone Meal to "grow" them:
- each selected card gains a permanent **+5 Chips**, and
- the **poker hand** they form is permanently buffed by **+5 Chips and +1 Mult** (like a partial hand level-up).

Consumed on use.

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table from a single **[[Bone]]**:

```
. . .
. B .
. . .
```
(B = Bone.) See [[Crafting]].

It can also be produced at the **[[Composter]]**, which turns organic items (flowers and farm plants) into Bone Meal.
