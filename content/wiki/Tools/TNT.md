## Effect
Select **1 card** in your hand, then use the TNT to **destroy it and the two cards on either side of it** (up to 3 cards total). Destruction is permanent, like The Hanged Man — Glass cards shatter. A handy way to clear unwanted cards from your deck. Consumed on use.

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table — Minecraft's gunpowder pattern around a Cobblestone core: **4 [[Gunpowder]] in the corners, 1 [[Cobblestone]] in the centre**:

```
g . g
. C .
g . g
```
(g = Gunpowder, C = Cobblestone.) A [[Gunpowder]] sink — see [[Crafting]].
