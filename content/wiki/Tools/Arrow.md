## Effect
Select **1 card** in your hand, then use the Arrow to **permanently give it +3 Mult**. A cheap, single-use way to sharpen a key card. Consumed on use.

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table — the Minecraft arrow shape: **[[Flint]] tip over a [[Sticks|Stick]] shaft over a [[Feather]] fletching**:

```
. F .
. S .
. f .
```
(F = Flint, S = Stick, f = Feather.) Part of the Archery crafting cluster — see [[Crafting]].
