## Effect
The **Nether key.** While you're in a **Ruined Portal** blind and holding at least **1 [[Obsidian]]**, use the Flint and Steel to **light the portal**: it spends 1 Obsidian, **instantly beats the blind**, warps the run to a random **Nether** biome, and rerolls this ante's boss to a Nether boss. Consumed on use. See [[Dimensions]].

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table — Minecraft's diagonal of **1 [[Iron]] + 1 [[Flint]]**:

```
I . .
. F .
. . .
```
(I = Iron, F = Flint.) Iron is smelt-only, so this is naturally gated behind a [[Smelting|Furnace]]. See [[Crafting]].
