*Craft-only Tool consumable — 6 material variants*

## Effect
When used, the Pickaxe **arms a one-shot**: the next time you **defeat a blind**, you gather **+N of a random ore** (on top of the normal drop). N scales with the material. It's a **persistent, multi-use** tool: usable **once per Blind**, it spends one **Use** each time and **breaks** when it runs out.

**Enchantable:** [[Fortune]] adds a random bonus to each haul; [[Durability]] raises its Uses. See [[Enchanting]].

> **Planned rescope:** once [[Ore Blocks]] ship, the Pickaxe will boost the ore you **mine from cards** (yield +N per mined ore) rather than arming a blind-defeat bonus. See [[Enhancements|Ore Blocks]].

## Recipe
Minecraft pickaxe shape — **3 of the material across the top, 2 [[Sticks|Sticks]] as the handle**:

```
M M M
. S .
. S .
```
(M = material ore, S = Stick.) See [[Crafting]].
