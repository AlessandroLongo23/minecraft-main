*Craft-only Tool — 6 material variants*

![[tool_pickaxe_diamond.png|105]]

## Effect
The Pickaxe **mines [[Mining|Ore Block]] cards**. Highlight the Ore Blocks in your hand, then **Use** the Pickaxe to mine the ones this tier can break — each yields its ore into your inventory and reverts to a plain card. It's a **persistent, multi-use** tool: usable **once per Blind**, it spends one **Use** per mining action and **breaks** when it runs out. At most **one Pickaxe** per Blind. (Selecting nothing minable doesn't waste a Use.)

Which ores you can break is gated by the material's **mining level**, faithful to Minecraft — including the famous **Golden Pickaxe quirk**: gold mines no better than wood. Wood Ore Blocks can't be mined by a pickaxe at all — use an [[Axe]] for those.

**Enchantable:** [[Enchanting Books/Fortune\|Fortune]] adds a random bonus to each ore mined (up to +1 / +2 / +3 by tier); [[Enchanting Books/Durability\|Durability]] raises its Uses. See [[Enchanting]].

## How to get it
Craft it at the 3×3 [[Crafting]] table. Minecraft pickaxe shape — **3 of the material across the top, 2 [[Sticks|Sticks]] as the handle**:

```
M M M
. S .
. S .
```
(M = material ore, S = Stick.)

> **Netherite is the exception.** Like in Minecraft, the Netherite Pickaxe can't be crafted at the table. Craft the **Diamond** Pickaxe, then **upgrade** it at the [[Anvil]] → Upgrade tab (Diamond tool + 1 [[Netherite]] → Netherite tool). The upgrade keeps the tool's enchants and durability.
