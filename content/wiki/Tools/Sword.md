*Craft-only Tool consumable — 6 material variants*

## Effect
When used, the Sword grants **×Mult on every hand for the rest of the current Blind** (bigger materials give a bigger multiplier), then **resets at the start of the next Blind**. It's a **persistent, multi-use** tool: usable **once per Blind**, it spends one **Use** each time and **breaks** when it runs out. Only **one Sword** can be used per Blind (you can still pair it with one Pickaxe and one Shovel). When it scores it animates like a Joker — a floating **×Mult** pop over the card on every hand.

**Enchantable:** [[Enchanting Books/Sharpness\|Sharpness]] multiplies its ×Mult (e.g. Diamond ×8 + Sharpness III = ×14); [[Durability]] raises its Uses. See [[Enchanting]].

```
. M .
. M .
. S .
```
