## Effect
Applying the Lapis Sheet turns the highlighted card into a **Lapis card**: it grants **[[Experience|XP]]** whenever it is **played**. It reuses the mod's existing Lapis enhancement — see **[[Enhancements|Lapis card]]** for the full details on the XP it gives.

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Lapis Lazuli|Lapis]]**.
