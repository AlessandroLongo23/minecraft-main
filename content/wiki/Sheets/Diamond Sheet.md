## Effect
Applying the Diamond Sheet turns the highlighted card into a **Diamond card**: it gives **X2 Mult** while it is **held in hand**. This is the premium held-in-hand multiplier — stronger than the [[Iron Sheet|Iron card]] (X1.5) and the [[Enhancements|Obsidian card]] (X1.25), which is why it's the most expensive to forge.

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Diamond]]**.
