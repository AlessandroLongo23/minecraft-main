## Effect
Applying the Iron Sheet turns the highlighted card into an **Iron card**: it gives **X1.5 Mult** while it is **held in hand**. (Under the hood this is Balatro's Steel enhancement, relabelled "Iron" throughout BalaCraft — Minecraft has no steel.)

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Iron]]**.
