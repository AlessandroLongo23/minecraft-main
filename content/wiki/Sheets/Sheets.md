---

A **Sheet** is a craft-only consumable you forge from ore and then **apply to a playing card** to give that card a Balatro **enhancement** — exactly the way a Tarot does. Sheets are BalaCraft's Minecraft-flavoured way to upgrade your deck cards: each one changes a card's center (an [[Enhancements|enhancement]], **not** an edition).

**Where you craft them**
- Most sheets are forged at the **[[Anvil]]** on its **Forge Sheets** tab — **2× of one ore → 1 Sheet**.
- The **[[Glass Sheet]]** is the exception: it's made at the **[[Crafting]]** table from 3 Glass.

**How you apply them**
A Sheet is used just like a Tarot. While selecting a hand, **highlight one card in your hand**, then highlight the Sheet and press **Use**. The card takes on the Sheet's enhancement (replacing any enhancement it already had). The Sheet is consumed.

See [[Resources]] for the ores you'll need, and [[Enhancements]] for the resulting card behaviours (Sheets are the *appliers*; Enhancements documents the card effects in detail).
