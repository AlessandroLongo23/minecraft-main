## Effect
Applying the Coal Sheet turns the highlighted card into a **Coal card**: it gives **+40 Chips** every time it **scores**. Coal is fuel, so it adds raw scoring energy. The cheapest sheet to forge.

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Coal]]**.
