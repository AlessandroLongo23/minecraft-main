## Effect
Applying the Emerald Sheet turns the highlighted card into an **Emerald card**: it earns you **$4** every time it **scores**. Fitting for emeralds, Minecraft's trade currency — turn a reliable scoring card into a money engine.

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Emerald]]**.
