## Effect
Applying the Gold Sheet turns the highlighted card into a **Gold card**: it earns **+$3** if it is still held in hand at the **end of the round**. A reliable, low-risk source of steady income for your deck.

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Gold]]**.
