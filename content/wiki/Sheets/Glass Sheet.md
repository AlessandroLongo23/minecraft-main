## Effect
Applying the Glass Sheet turns the highlighted card into a **Glass card**: it gives **X2 Mult** when scored, but has a **1-in-4 chance to shatter** (destroy itself) each time it scores. High reward, fragile.

## How to craft it
- Unlike the other sheets, the Glass Sheet is **not** forged at the Anvil — it's made at the **[[Crafting]]** table.
- Costs **3× [[Glass]]**.
