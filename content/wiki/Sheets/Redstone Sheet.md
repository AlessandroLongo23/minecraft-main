## Effect
Applying the Redstone Sheet turns the highlighted card into a **Redstone card**: it **retriggers once** when scored — its scoring effects all fire an extra time, just like a Red seal. Redstone is the repeating signal of Minecraft, so the card fires twice.

## How to craft it
- Forge it at the **[[Anvil]]** on the **Forge Sheets** tab.
- Costs **2× [[Redstone]]**.
