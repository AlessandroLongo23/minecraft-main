*Edition — the card side of the Durability book*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Lane | Protection |
| Applied by | the dual-target [[Enchanting Books/Durability\|Durability book]] (highlight a card in hand + the book → **Enchant Card**) |
| Apply cost | 2 [[Experience\|levels]] (flat — any book tier) |
| Internal key | `e_balacraft_unbreaking` |

## Effect
A card with the **Unbreaking** glint is protected:
- It **can't be debuffed** by boss [[New Bosses|blinds]] (suit/rank debuffs, "debuff enhanced cards", etc.).
- If it's also a **Glass** card, it **won't break** when it would normally shatter.

The MC durability enchantment, turned into card protection — best on your key scorers or fragile Glass
cards. **Flat:** any tier of Durability book gives full protection (the tier only matters on tools).

## Notes
- Applied via the [[Enchanting]] flow (select card + book → **Enchant Card** → spend levels). Gated by
  the [[Enchanting Table]] voucher.
- The applier is the **dual-target [[Enchanting Books/Durability|Durability book]]**: on a **tool** it
  raises max Uses, on a **card** it applies this Unbreaking edition. One book, two targets.
- Editions are mutually exclusive — a card holds one of Sharpness / Unbreaking / Lucky at a time.
