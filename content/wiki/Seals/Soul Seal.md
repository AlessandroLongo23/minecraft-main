## Effect
When a card with the **Soul Seal** is **played and scores**, you gain **+5 [[Experience|XP]]**.

It's the "play" trigger of the seal trio (alongside [[Cooked Seal]] = held, [[Ore Seal]] =
discard). Where the [[Lapis]] enhancement also grants XP on play, the Soul Seal is the rarer,
seal-applied version and grants a little more.

## Notes
- Stacks with everything else the card does when it scores.
- Feeds the [[Experience]] bar, which you spend on [[Enchanting]].
