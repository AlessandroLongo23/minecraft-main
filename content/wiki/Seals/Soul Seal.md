*Seal — the "on play" trigger*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | **On play** (when the card scores) |
| Lane | [[Experience]] |
| Applied by | the **Soul Seal** Spectral card |
| Internal key | `balacraft_soul` |

## Effect
When a card with the **Soul Seal** is **played and scores**, you gain **+5 [[Experience|XP]]**.

It's the "play" trigger of the seal trio (alongside [[Cooked Seal]] = held, [[Ore Seal]] = discard).

## Notes
- A flat +5 XP, so it's strongest early on; the [[Lapis]] enhancement also grants XP on play but scales
  with your level (≈ a quarter-level each). A card can hold a Lapis enhancement **and** a Soul Seal at
  once for two XP sources.
- Stacks with everything else the card does when it scores.
- Applied by the **Soul Seal** Spectral card (rolls in the shop and in Spectral packs).
- Feeds the [[Experience]] bar, which you spend at the [[Enchanting Table]] for [[Enchanting Books]].
