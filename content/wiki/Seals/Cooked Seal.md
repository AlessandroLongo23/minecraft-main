## Effect
A card with the **Cooked Seal** that is still **in your hand at the end of the round** "cooks" into a
**[[Food]] consumable** — a random food is created in a free consumable slot. A small modal then asks
what to do with the card:

- **Keep cooking** — leave the seal on the card, so it cooks again next round.
- **Eat the seal** — remove the seal but keep the card.
- **Burn it** — destroy the card entirely.

This is the MC-native counterpart to the vanilla **Blue Seal** (which makes a Planet if held). Instead
of a Planet, you get something to manage your [[Hunger]].

## Notes
- Needs a free consumable slot to create the food, same as Blue Seal needs room for a Planet.
- Pairs with the [[Hunger]] system: a renewable food source if you keep re-cooking.
