*Seal — the "held in hand" trigger*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Held in hand at **end of round** |
| Lane | [[Food]] |
| Applied by | the **Cooked Seal** Spectral card |
| Internal key | `balacraft_cooked` |

## Effect
A card with the **Cooked Seal** that is still **in your hand at the end of the round** "cooks" into a
**random [[Food]] consumable** — created in a free slot, deferred just like the vanilla **Blue Seal**
makes a Planet. The seal **stays on the card** and cooks again **every round**, so it's a renewable food
source.

This is the MC-native counterpart to the Blue Seal: instead of a Planet, you get something to manage your
[[Hunger]].

## Notes
- Needs room for a consumable to create the food, same as the Blue Seal needs room for a Planet.
- Applied by the **Cooked Seal** Spectral card (rolls in the shop and in Spectral packs).
- Pairs with the [[Hunger]] system — keep the card in hand each round for a steady food supply.
