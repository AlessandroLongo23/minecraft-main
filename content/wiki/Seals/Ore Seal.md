## Effect
When a card with the **Ore Seal** is **discarded**, you **mine 1 [[Resources|resource]]**. The ore is
rolled the same way blind drops are: from the current [[Biomes|biome]]'s bias, at the ante's unlocked
tier (tier 1 from ante 1, tier 2 from ante 3, tier 3 from ante 6).

This is the MC-native counterpart to the vanilla **Purple Seal** (which makes a Tarot on discard) —
instead of a Tarot, you get ore for [[Crafting]].

## Notes
- Draws from the same ore pool as [[Resources|blind drops]] and the [[Mining Tag]], so your biome
  decides *which* ore.
- Different from [[Ore Blocks]] (an enhancement you mine by *playing* the card): the Ore Seal fires on
  **discard**.
