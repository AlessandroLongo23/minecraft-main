*Seal — the "on discard" trigger*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | **On discard** |
| Lane | [[Resources]] |
| Applied by | the **Ore Seal** Spectral card |
| Internal key | `balacraft_ore` |

## Effect
When a card with the **Ore Seal** is **discarded**, you **mine 1 random ore**. The ore is rolled the same
way blind drops are: weighted by your current [[Biomes|biome]]'s bias, at the ante's unlocked tier.

This is the MC-native counterpart to the vanilla **Purple Seal** (which makes a Tarot on discard) —
instead of a Tarot, you get an ore for [[Crafting]].

## Notes
- Draws from the same ore pool as [[Resources|blind drops]] and the [[Mining Tag]], so your biome decides
  *which* ore you get.
- Different from [[Ore Blocks]] (an enhancement you [[Mining|mine]] with a [[Pickaxe]]): the Ore Seal
  fires on **discard**, not on play.
- Applied by the **Ore Seal** Spectral card (rolls in the shop and in Spectral packs).
