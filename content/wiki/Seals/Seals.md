Seals are small stamps applied to a **playing card**. Vanilla Balatro seals are *generators*
(Blue → Planet, Purple → Tarot on discard, Gold → $); BalaCraft adds three MC-native seals that
generate **BalaCraft** things instead — one for each trigger (held in hand · discarded · played),
each tied to a different subsystem.

The base game's four seals (Gold / Red / Blue / Purple) still work normally; these are **added**
alongside them.

| Seal | Trigger | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Cooked Seal]] | held in hand at end of round | cooks the card into a [[Food]] card |
| [[Ore Seal]] | on discard | mine a [[Resources\|resource]] |
| [[Soul Seal]] | on play | gain [[Experience\|XP]] |

## Applying seals
Like vanilla seals, these are applied by **Spectral cards** (one new spectral per seal — names TBD).
The base game already applies its own seals via spectrals (Deja Vu, Trance, Talisman, Medium); ours
follow the same pattern.
