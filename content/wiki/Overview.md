**BalaCraft** is a Balatro mod based on the very best videogame, Minecraft.

BalaCraft adds lots of new jokers to the game, along with new decks, blinds, bosses and vouchers, and several entirely new subsystems — resource **gathering**, **mining**, **crafting**, **smelting**, **enchanting**, **brewing**, food/**hunger**, hearts/**health**, **experience**, **villager trading**, and **biomes/dimensions**. Every addition is made to preserve the original feeling of both games, so that the vanilla-like feel of Balatro is not compromised and the Minecraft experience everyone had is portrayed in the new cards and mechanics of this mod.

> This wiki documents the content **actually implemented in the mod** (what loads and ships). Where something is designed but not yet enabled, it is flagged **(planned)**.

## How content keys work

Every BalaCraft item is registered through Steamodded with the mod prefix `balacraft`, so internal keys look like `j_balacraft_waterdrop` (joker), `bl_balacraft_creeper` (blind), `c_balacraft_tool_iron_sword` (tool consumable), `c_balacraft_potion_healing` (potion), `b_balacraft_nether` (deck), `v_balacraft_enchanting_table` (voucher), `m_balacraft_lapis` (card enhancement). You don't need these to play — they're listed in each entry for reference/debugging.
