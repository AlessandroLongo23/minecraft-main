| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rarity | Rare |
| Cost | $3 |

## Effect
A novelty/joke card. Its tooltip simply reads *"Probability of finding this is 0.164%."* It has no scoring effect — its whole gimmick is its **$64 sell value** (set when it enters your jokers).

## Notes
- The high sell value comes from an `extra_value` bonus applied when the joker is added to your deck, on top of the normal half-of-cost sell price.
- **Not** Blueprint / Brainstorm compatible. Eternal / Perishable compatible.
- The name references the in-game rarity flavor — it does not actually change drop odds.
