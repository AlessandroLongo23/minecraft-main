## Effect
A precision scaling engine — it rewards taking a single "clean shot" each round.

- **At end of round, gains ×0.25 Mult** if you played **exactly one hand** that round (`Bullseye!`).
- The accumulated multiplier (tooltip *"Currently ×N Mult"*) is applied whenever the joker scores, once it has grown past ×1.

## Notes
- Spending discards is fine — only **playing** more than one hand forfeits that round's bonus.
- Blueprint / Brainstorm / Eternal / Perishable compatible. A copy applies the X-Mult but does not double-charge the accumulation.

## How to get it
The Bow is a **persistent joker** crafted on the [[Crafting]] grid (it is not sold in shops):

- **3× [[Sticks]] + 3× [[String]]**

Gather [[String]] from mob drops and craft [[Sticks]] from Wood. See [[Resources]] for where each ingredient comes from. Owning a Bow this run is the prerequisite for crafting the [[Crossbow]].
