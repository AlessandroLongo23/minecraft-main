## Effect
A "loaded shot" rhythm engine — it charges on one hand, then fires on the next:

- **Every other scored hand fires for ×3 Mult** (`Fire!`).
- The hands in between **reload** (`Reloading...`) and apply no bonus.
- Each round starts fresh in the charging state, so a charge cannot be banked across rounds.

## Notes
- **Not** Blueprint / Brainstorm compatible (the loaded toggle would desync under a copy). Eternal / Perishable compatible.

## How to get it
The Crossbow is a **persistent joker** crafted on the [[Crafting]] grid (it is not sold in shops), and has a prerequisite:

- **3× [[String]] + 2× [[Sticks]] + 1× [[Iron]]**
- You must already **own a [[Bow]] this run** — it is the upgrade end of the Archery line.

[[Iron]] is furnace-smelted, so the Crossbow naturally comes online after you have a Furnace. See [[Resources]] and [[Crafting]] for ingredient sources and the crafting flow.
