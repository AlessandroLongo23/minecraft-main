## Effect
An economy / loot engine — each cash-out **reels in** a random reward:

- **55%** — an ore (a random [[Resources|gathered ore]], scaled to your current ante).
- **30%** — **$4**.
- **15%** — a **mob drop** (falls back to $4 if no mob drop exists at that tier).

The reward fires at end of round (`Reeled ...!`).

## Notes
- The ore / mob-drop tier scales with the ante you are on.
- **Not** Blueprint / Brainstorm compatible (it mutates RNG and your resource/economy state). Eternal / Perishable compatible.

## How to get it
The Fishing Rod is a **persistent joker** crafted on the [[Crafting]] grid (it is not sold in shops):

- **3× [[Sticks]] + 2× [[String]]**

Craft [[Sticks]] from Wood and gather [[String]] from mob drops — see [[Resources]] for sources.
