BalaCraft adds the **enchanting voucher pair** — [[Enchanting Table]] and [[Sorcerer's Tome]] — which unlock and upgrade the [[Enchanting]] system, and each grant **+1 consumable slot**.

| Voucher | Cost | Effect | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Enchanting Table]] | $10 | Unlock [[Enchanting Books]] + [[Enchanting Pack]]; +1 consumable slot | Available from the start |
| [[Sorcerer's Tome]] | $12 | Books appear more often + Tier III unlock; +1 consumable slot | Only after an Enchanting Table this run |

> **Portal vouchers were removed.** Dimensions are no longer reached by buying a voucher — you travel mid-run via the Ruined Portal blind (light it with [[Flint and Steel]] + Obsidian for the Nether) and the [[Eye of Ender]] trail (for the End). See [[Dimensions]].
