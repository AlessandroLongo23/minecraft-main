> ⚠️ **This is a developer/testing deck.** It exists so the mod's content (jokers, vouchers, enchanting, crafting, resources) can be exercised immediately without grinding a full run. It is intended to be **removed before any public release**, and it replaces the old "Overworld" starting deck. For a normal Overworld start, use a [[Biome Decks|biome deck]] instead.

## Effect
On run start it force-grants a sandbox loadout:
- **+$999** starting cash.
- **XP level 100** (see the [[Experience|XP]] system).
- **A full stack (16) of every resource** — gathered ores *and* crafted intermediates — with the inventory enlarged so it all fits, plus spare slots.
- **Both enchanting vouchers pre-redeemed**: [[Enchanting Table]] and [[Sorcerer's Tome]] (so books, the Enchanting Pack, and the extra consumable slots are active from turn one).

The exact amounts are a handful of tunable knobs in the deck's source — handy for testing a specific scenario.

## How to reach the Nether / End from here
Dimensions are no longer voucher-warped. The sandbox grant stocks Obsidian, Ender Pearls and Blaze Powder; craft [[Flint and Steel]] and [[Eye of Ender|Eyes of Ender]] at the Crafting Table, then travel via the Ruined Portal blind / the Eye-of-Ender trail. See [[Dimensions]].
