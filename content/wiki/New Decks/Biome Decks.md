*Start-in-biome decks — one per Overworld biome (8 variants)*

![[deck_biomes_sheet.png|320]]

There is **one deck per Overworld biome**. Each makes the run begin in that specific biome (instead of a random one), letting you build around its ore bias and arrival bonus. See [[Biomes]] for each biome's details.

| Deck | Starts you in | Arrival $ | Ore bias | Internal key |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plains Deck | [[Plains]] | — | Wood, Cobblestone | `b_balacraft_plains` |
| Forest Deck | [[Forest]] | — | Wood, Coal | `b_balacraft_forest` |
| Desert Deck | [[Desert]] | +$4 | Gold, Iron | `b_balacraft_desert` |
| Snowy Taiga Deck | [[Snowy Taiga]] | −$2 | Diamond, Iron | `b_balacraft_snowy_taiga` |
| Jungle Deck | [[Jungle]] | — | Wood, Coal | `b_balacraft_jungle` |
| Swamp Deck | [[Swamp]] | −$1 | Coal, Wood | `b_balacraft_swamp` |
| Savanna Deck | [[Savanna]] | +$2 | Cobblestone, Gold | `b_balacraft_savanna` |
| Badlands Deck | [[Badlands]] | +$3 | Gold, Iron | `b_balacraft_badlands` |

## Effect
Each deck calls "start in biome X," which sets your dimension to the Overworld and applies that biome's tint and arrival modifier on turn one. The arrival $ (above) is the biome's one-time on-enter bonus/penalty.

> **Plains Deck** additionally starts you with **2 Torches** — this is a dev/testing convenience (handy for trying out [[Crafting]] and the [[Torch]]) and may be removed.

## Unlock
Each biome deck unlocks after you **win 10 antes (boss blinds) in that biome**, counted cumulatively across all runs. Nether and End biomes don't have their own decks — reach those via the [[Nether Deck]] / [[End Deck]], or mid-run through the structure-based [[Dimensions]] travel.
