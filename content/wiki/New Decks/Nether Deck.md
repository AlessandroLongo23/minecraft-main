## Effect
The run begins in the **Nether** dimension — you start in a random Nether biome (Nether Wastes, Crimson Forest, Soul Sand Valley, or Basalt Deltas) instead of the Overworld, with that dimension's biome pool, ore biases and exclusive boss for the rest of the run's biome rolls. See [[Biomes]].

## Unlock
**Enter the Nether a cumulative 10 times across your runs.** You reach the Nether mid-run by lighting a **Ruined Portal** blind with [[Flint and Steel]] (which spends an Obsidian) — see [[Dimensions]]. Each entry ticks a persistent counter; hitting 10 permanently unlocks this deck. (The portal *vouchers* that used to feed this unlock were removed.)
