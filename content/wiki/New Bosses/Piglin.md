## Effect
**After scoring, every card you played is turned into a 2 (suit kept) — unless your deck contains at least 2 Gold-enhanced cards**, which "bribes" the piglins and cancels the effect. Carry Gold cards to appease them.

## Notes
- The rank conversion happens after the scoring animation, so it degrades your deck for the rest of the run, not just this hand.
- Renders with the base-game blind-chip sprite until dedicated art is added.
