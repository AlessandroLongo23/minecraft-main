*Nether boss blind*

![[boss_blaze.png|120]]

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Appears from | Ante 2 |
| Score requirement | Standard boss (×2) |
| Resource drop | Tier-roll (2× of a random ore, tier scales with ante) |
| Mob attack | 2 ♥ (1 heart) on a failed hand |
| Internal key | `bl_balacraft_blaze` |

## Effect
**After each hand is scored, every card in your hand has a 1-in-5 (20%) chance to be burned (destroyed).** Over several hands this steadily thins your hand.

## Notes
- The tooltip says "each card in played hand," but the effect actually rolls on the cards **left in your hand** after scoring.
