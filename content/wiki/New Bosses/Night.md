*Environment blind — the reskinned Small Blind (surface)*

**Night** is BalaCraft's reskin of the vanilla **Small Blind** — the **surface** environment. It stays mechanically vanilla (no debuff, still skippable, same chip requirement and reward); only the name and sprite change.

What it drives is the **environment-specific drop pool**: beating a Night blind leans toward **surface** materials. Alongside the usual ore roll you can pick up the surface mob drops — [[String]], [[Feather]], [[Flint]], [[Gunpowder]], [[Bone]] and the surface-only [[Leather]]. See [[Resource Drops]].

Night and Cave blinds can also be **re-themed as Minecraft structures** each ante (Desert Temple, Shipwreck, the Ruined Portal, …) — see [[Dimensions]].
