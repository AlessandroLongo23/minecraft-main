## Effect
**Before your second hand, you lose $ equal to the total rank of the cards you played in your first hand.** Your opening hand is recorded; when you play your second hand, that dollar amount is deducted first. A big, high-rank opener can bleed a lot of money — so a cheap first hand keeps the toll low.
