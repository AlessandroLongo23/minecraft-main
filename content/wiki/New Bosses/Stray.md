*Overworld boss blind*

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Appears from | Ante 2 |
| Score requirement | Standard boss (×2) |
| Resource drop | Tier-roll (2× of a random ore, tier scales with ante) |
| Mob attack | **3 ♥ (1.5 hearts)** on a failed hand |
| Internal key | `bl_balacraft_stray` |

## Effect
**Your Mult is capped at the Mult of your previous played hand.** The first hand of the round is uncapped; from then on, each hand's Mult can't exceed the value your last hand produced — so you can't keep ramping a single big hand. The newly-capped value becomes the cap for the next hand.
