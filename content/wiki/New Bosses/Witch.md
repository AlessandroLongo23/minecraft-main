## Effect
**At the start of the round the Witch "shuffles" your hands and discards** — it keeps the *total* number of hands + discards the same but randomly re-splits it between the two (always leaving you at least 1 hand). You might enter the fight with extra hands and no discards, or vice-versa.

## Notes
- Reverted if the blind is disabled. Renders with the base-game blind-chip sprite until dedicated art is added.
