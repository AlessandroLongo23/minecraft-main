*Overworld boss blind*

![[boss_phantom.png|120]]

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Appears from | Ante 2 |
| Score requirement | **×3** if you skipped no blind this ante (else standard ×2) |
| Resource drop | Tier-roll (2× of a random ore, tier scales with ante) |
| Mob attack | 2 ♥ (1 heart) on a failed hand |
| Internal key | `bl_balacraft_phantom` |

## Effect
**The score requirement is tripled (×3) if you did not skip any blind this ante.** Skipping at least one Small/Big blind earlier in the ante leaves Phantom at the normal boss requirement — a nod to phantoms only attacking players who haven't slept.

## Notes
- The "did you skip a blind this ante?" counter is tracked by a source patch and reset each ante.
