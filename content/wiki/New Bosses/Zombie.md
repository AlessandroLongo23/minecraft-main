| Internal key | `bl_balacraft_zombie` |

## Effect
**After you beat it, your leftmost joker becomes Perishable** (it will expire after the normal number of rounds). The infection lands on whichever joker is in the leftmost slot when the blind is defeated, so you can re-order your jokers beforehand to choose the victim.
