*Overworld boss blind*

![[boss_spider.png|120]]

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Appears from | Ante 2 |
| Score requirement | Standard boss (×2) |
| Resource drop | Tier-roll (2× of a random ore, tier scales with ante) |
| Mob attack | 2 ♥ (1 heart) on a failed hand |
| Internal key | `bl_balacraft_spider` |

## Effect
**Debuffs every card that has an enhancement.** Any playing card whose face isn't the plain base card (Bonus, Mult, Wild, Glass, Steel, Gold, Stone, Lucky, etc.) is debuffed for the fight — so enhancement-heavy decks are hit hardest.

## Notes
- The whole card is debuffed (there's no way to neutralize *just* the enhancement), so its base chips/suit don't score either while it's debuffed.
