Each showdown boss spawns as the current Ante Boss (substituting the previous one) under specific conditions:

| Boss           | Condition                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ender Dragon   | Entering the End biome.                                                                                         |
| Wither         | Possessing 3\* wither skulls and 4\* blocks of soul sand.                                                       |
| Elder Guardian | Drinking a Potion of water breathing while in the Ocean biome (the Ante Boss will also host an Ocean Monument). |
| Warden         | Beating both the small blind and the big blind with the first hand of the round while in the Ancient City.      |
\* Amount subject to change
