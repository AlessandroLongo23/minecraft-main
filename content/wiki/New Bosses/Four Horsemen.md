## Effect
The nastiest of the End blinds — at the start of the round it hits you with four punishments at once:
- **Debuffs 6 random cards** in your deck for the fight.
- **−1 hand** this round.
- **−1 discard** this round.
- **Destroys all of your consumables.**

Everything except the destroyed consumables is reverted when the blind ends.
