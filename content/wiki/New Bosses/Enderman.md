## Effect
**Teleports a random joker to the leftmost slot.** It pins one joker to the left at the start of the round and **re-pins a random joker after every played hand**, scrambling any joker order that depends on position. Only one joker is pinned at a time, and the effect needs at least 2 jokers to do anything. The pin is cleared once the blind ends.

## Notes
- Discards and the initial draw don't re-pin — only *playing* a hand does.
