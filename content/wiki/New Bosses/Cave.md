*Environment blind — the reskinned Big Blind (underground)*

**Cave** is BalaCraft's reskin of the vanilla **Big Blind** — the **underground** environment. It stays mechanically vanilla (no debuff, still skippable, same chip requirement and reward); only the name and sprite change.

Beating a Cave blind leans toward **underground** materials: the shared mob drops ([[String]], [[Feather]], [[Flint]], [[Gunpowder]], [[Bone]]) plus the **cave-only** [[Spider Eye]] and [[Glow Ink Sac]], and the occasional [[Resources/Obsidian|Obsidian]]. See [[Resource Drops]].

Night and Cave blinds can also be **re-themed as Minecraft structures** each ante — see [[Dimensions]].
